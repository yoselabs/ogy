Start every compact card with an icon and its humanized title; put the ontology kind in a quiet right corner. Reveal children only when their meaning can be read. Keep the existing canvas, walk and recorded purpose chain.

## 1. Findings

1. **The same relationship has two names.** `lend-tools → tools-reach-members` is `realizes` in `graph_tool.json:1 /edges/0`. The board prints “serves goal” (`lab28.tpl.html:300`), while the capability card prints “REALIZES” (`:110`). Use `realizes` everywhere in the forward direction and `← realizes` in the reverse direction until a declared inverse label exists. This is particularly misleading because `capability` explicitly renamed `serves` to `realizes` (`K/capability.yml:16–17`); `serves` still has a different, valid meaning on an interface (`K/interface.yml:24`).

2. **Goal and capability cards spend attention on scaffolding.** The board allocates 280px width, a separate kind line, title, full sentence and all relation rows (`lab28.tpl.html:111,167–168`); the card repeats a large icon tile and heading (`:164,468`, CSS `:67`). Use a 240–320px compact card with one title line, a corner kind word and at most one useful next-level summary. Full sentences and relation inventories belong in the opened detail view.

3. **There is already a registry, but it does not own every rendering decision.** `VIEW`, shared mention functions and board dispatch exist (`lab28.tpl.html:92–139,390`); hand-built headings, actor summaries, pill text and call explanations remain (`:154–168,237–258,345,395–414,460–478`). Extend this system rather than build a second one. `namedHtml` is a no-op, so it does not repair free-text names (`:451–452`).

4. **The Member / Mail relay inconsistency has a precise cause.** Member is a human actor, Mail relay a program actor (`graph_tool.json:1 /nodes/0,/nodes/3`). Notify's `send` step has **no actor edge**; its `ok` case reads Mail relay and has two `tell` effects, one on Mail relay and one on Member (`/nodes/62,/nodes/64`, `/edges/43–48`). The same actor therefore legitimately appears in two roles. `pillsOf` creates separate read/effect items (`lab28.tpl.html:251–253`), but `screenSvg0` renders the effect mention with `code:true`, leaving `mail-relay` and `member` raw, while the read mention becomes “Mail relay” (`:136,414`). It also discards the operation glyph in favor of the target's kind icon. The identity path is partly shared; **a caller-controlled naming escape hatch and missing role labels** are the immediate faults. Render a single “Mail relay” mention with adjacent `when.reads` and `effects · tell` role marks; render “Member” with `effects · tell`. Do not deduplicate different relations out of existence.

5. **Do not explain Notify as being taken by Mail relay.** That would confuse the recipient with the actor who acts on a step. This template has “Started by” (`lab28.tpl.html:237`), but no literal “taken by”. Notify is reached by Remind's `calls`; Mail relay occurs in Notify's condition and effects, not its `step.actor` (`graph_tool.json:1 /edges/43–55`). An older screenshot or paraphrase cannot establish a current label. The redesigned header says `← calls: Remind`; the case shows the two roles above. “The mailer takes the message” is authored step prose (`/nodes/64`), not an actor attribution.

6. **A step is being mistaken for a screen, and a system step for a decision.** Browser dots, form inputs, “no screen”, and a 17px question diamond are drawn by `screenSvg0` (`lab28.tpl.html:397–412`); layout chooses a compact system block by absence of actor/call/interface (`:266`). The ontology explicitly says a step is not a wizard screen (`K/step.yml:2–5`), and a decision is an owner question, not automated evaluation (`K/decision.yml:3–6`). Draw a step as a step; attach actual interfaces through `serves`. Use a small branch mark only when there are multiple cases. Reserve the decision glyph for a decision card.

7. **The current detail threshold hides bodies without replacing their meaning.** Steps render only above global `scale > 0.12` (`lab28.tpl.html:351`), although internal edges are built independently (`:376–384`) and entity internals have no matching threshold (`:353–361`). Use local screen-space gates and remove hidden child paths/hit targets together. Replace them with counts and typed relation summaries; roll cross-boundary edges to visible representatives.

8. **A call already contains both handoff and outcome handling.** `step.calls`, child cases' `covers_ends` and `then` supply the necessary structure (`K/step.yml:17`, `K/case.yml:22–23`, `K/workflow.yml:19–20`). Remind names Notify's success and failure ends and continues to the same `done` end in both cases (`graph_tool.json:1 /edges/49–55`). Split the *drawing* into `calls` and `cases / covers_ends / then` inside the same step. An extra “wait for reply” step is not necessary and would suggest new behavior. The prototype already gates the walk until a callee end returns (`lab28.tpl.html:504,509–511`).

9. **Some desired views cannot be truthful from these exports alone.** `proto2.py:8–21` flattens sentences into `says`, truncates at 400 characters, renames `type → atype`, `invoked_as → invoked`, and exports only selected scalar fields. It omits separate `title`, quantity `is/unit`, setting `name`, dependency `pin`, decision `context`, proposal `why/heard/sets`, and answer `said/at/note/where`. Both snapshots contain decisions/options/proposals but no answer nodes (`graph2.json:1 /nodes`, `graph_tool.json:1 /nodes`). A `chose` edge alone cannot show whether an option was answered or only proposed; answer provenance and the latest `at` matter (`K/answer.yml:2,13–18`, `K/proposal.yml:3–4`). Show “not exported” where needed; never interpret absent export fields as absent KB facts.

   This matters beyond the small fixture: `graph2.json:1 /nodes` contains 830 nodes, including 116 decisions, 279 options and 116 proposals, alongside 13 workflows, 14 capabilities and five goals. Current board creation supports workflow/entity/goal/capability/advice blocks (`lab28.tpl.html:111,242,278,281,295–302`); Search filters those blocks rather than all nodes (`:534`). Dedicated decision views and all-kind search are therefore a meaningful coverage improvement, not just restyling an already discoverable card.

10. **Reuse the agreed visual language, but keep its limits explicit.** Adopt the lifecycle-slice effect badge and unused-transition marker (`fable-picks.md:36–52,73–94`), actor cards, humanized names and off-view tooltips. Existing actor cards, mention humanization and viewport suppression are real starting points (`lab28.tpl.html:136–139,154,471,480–487`), but suppression checks only whole board blocks. A lifecycle diagram describes permitted moves, not current instance state (`K/transition.yml:2–3`; `spike-lifecycles.md:19,25–29`). A walk is inspection, not condition evaluation or effect execution (`lab28.tpl.html:510,516`; `decisions-verdict.md:30`).

## 2. Level model

Use **semantic zoom with C4's discipline of a distinct question at each scale**. C4 names context, container, component and code diagrams; it does not require every level. Borrow that separation of attention, without renaming a goal a “container” or pretending product/software are zoom levels. [C4 primary description](https://c4model.com/diagrams). The levels below are a viewer policy over ontology structure, not new ontology kinds.

### 2.1 Derivation before layout

1. Read layers and their direction from `O:7–9`; group by real area addresses (`K/area.yml:2–9`). A software area is not a child level of a product area. It can project links back to product. Layer selection is orthogonal to detail, with labelled boundary stubs for filtered targets.
2. Derive structural containment from `shape`, `part_of`, `key`, `holds` in kind descriptors: workflow → steps/ends → cases/choices/timeout → effects; entity → attributes/states/transitions; decision → options/proposal/answers; dependency → workarounds (`K/workflow.yml:9–26`; `K/step.yml:6–9`; `K/case.yml:6–8`; `K/effect.yml:6–9`; `K/entity.yml:11`; `K/attribute.yml:7–10`; `K/state.yml:6–9`; `K/transition.yml:6–8`; `K/decision.yml:10–23`; `K/option.yml:6–9`; `K/proposal.yml:7–10`; `K/answer.yml:6–9`; `K/workaround.yml:7–10`). These are the genuine reveal boundaries.
3. Derive the motivation projection from **typed** `workflow.realizes → capability.realizes → goal` and `goal.for → actor`, never from arbitrary graph reachability (`K/workflow.yml:35`; `K/capability.yml:16`; `K/goal.yml:14`). `dependency.realizes → actor` has a different typed meaning (`K/dependency.yml:17`). Do not mix these because they share a word.
4. `realizes` is a relation, not ownership. A capability may realize several goals (`K/capability.yml:16`). Keep one canonical object and use lightweight references in other goal contexts. A viewer focus frame can show the referenced capability completely, but it cannot change its parent or area. Unlinked objects remain visible under their area with “purpose not recorded”; do not manufacture a goal. `decisions-verdict.md:25,34` requires typed derived chains and exposed gaps.
5. Derive behavior ordering from `flow` metadata and actual `start/then/calls/covers_ends`, preserving loops and ordered cases (`K/workflow.yml:9–26`; `K/case.yml:2,20`). Layout rank is not a level. Summaries traverse with visited IDs; recursive calls get a cycle marker and an Open action, never infinite nesting.
6. Infer supporting views from actual `serves/offers/accepts/shows/includes/allows`, `performs/uses/satisfies/constrains`, `when.reads`, `derived.reads`, `effects.do/on`, `because` and `proof/src` (`K/interface.yml:24–30`; `K/handler.yml:11–14`; `K/rule.yml:3,16`; `K/attribute.yml:24`; `K/effect.yml:11–12`; `O:17–19`). Preserve raw witnesses. Purpose, rationale (`because`) and evidence (`src/proof`) are separate sections even if paths intersect.

### 2.4 Roll-up without semantic lies

For each fact, retain source ID, ontology field/path, target ID, direction, operation if any, and witness IDs. Resolve each endpoint to its nearest **visible structural owner**. When a purpose projection shows a referenced object inside another object, use its explicit visible reference identity; never pretend the relation created structural parentage. If no visible owner exists because of a filter, route to a labelled area/layer boundary stub. An offscreen but present node gets a directional stub rather than being treated as missing.

Group by `(source representative, target representative, ontology field/path, direction, operation, overlay status)`. A `when.reads` and a `tell` to Mail relay cannot become one anonymous arrow. Count unique original facts, not repeated rendering occurrences or every possible path through a cycle. For `effects`, use `(row ID, effect index)`; for plain links use `(source, field, target)`; for derived chains use their ordered witness tuple. A collapsed internal self-bundle becomes `N internal relations` on the frame, not a loop mistaken for a declared self-link. A true recursive `calls` remains an explicit recursive call marker.

Return-shaped connectors are the reverse **display** of `case.covers_ends → end`: bundle with direction metadata and label `← covers_ends`. A local `then` connector remains separate even when several cases continue to one end. Clicking any bundle lists exact source/target mentions and the field that each asserts. A count badge is viewer metadata, not an ontology edge claiming an ancestor has the child's field.

### 2.5 What derivation cannot choose

The ontology gives identities, parts, allowable links, flow roles and sentences. It cannot choose the user's current question, screen capacity, icon size, placement, which many-to-many reference is initially expanded, or whether an interface contract should look like a web page. Those are viewer defaults with optional symbolic kind hints (§5). Do not derive semantic depth by arbitrary shortest-path distance. Area, layer, purpose, workflow structure, data structure and rationale are different projections; a pinned chain can connect them without turning them into one tree.

## 3. View registry

Use the **Presentation Model** pattern: state, resolved identity and contextual decisions live outside HTML/SVG controls. This supports several views of the same data with fewer decisions in individual renderers. [Fowler's primary description](https://martinfowler.com/eaaDev/PresentationModel.html). The registry is the dispatch mechanism over those models; HTML and SVG are output adapters, not separate semantic implementations.

### 3.1 Contract every view receives

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `id`, `key`, `kind`, `kindWord` | Stable full identity, raw key and exact ontology kind; never use title as a lookup key |

### 3.3 Single mention path and naming rules

`entry identity → kind presentation model → context fact selection → mention token → HTML/SVG adapter`. The model owns title, canonical icon, color, kind, destination and accessible label. The caller supplies layout bounds and an optional role, **never a new title or code flag**. A role is rendered beside the mention, not baked into its name. Effect badge primitives compose entity/state/attribute mention tokens; they never concatenate raw keys. Title headings and search rows consume the same identity token in a heading variant.

- Use common `title` exactly when present (`O:16`); for terms use `word` (`K/term.yml:16`). Otherwise replace hyphens/underscores in a key with spaces, uppercase only its first letter, preserve the rest. Examples: Tools reach members; Lend tools; Close loan; Reminder bot; Mail relay. Do not lowercase an acronym or title-case each word. Explicit titles settle deliberate acronyms/proper spelling.
- Humanize references even inside type/effect badges: `of: Tool condition` names an enum, while `type: text` is a literal primitive token. Keep literal commands, routes, setting `name`, IDs, code examples and enum member values exact in a **separate literal renderer**. An entry is never a literal merely because an effect targets it. Raw key/ID remains available in details and copy-address.
- Do not transform `says/question/label/when` to manufacture identity. Preserve authored text and punctuation; wrap it without `clauses` rewriting. Do not automatically replace words matching entity names in prose: “member” as ordinary grammar is not necessarily a link. Structured references use tokens. Tooltips do not compete with a readable representation already on screen.
- All resolved entries are openable, including nested parts and entries without board blocks. Link target resolution first locates the visible owner, reveals the part if possible, and opens the inspector. Self-identity headings need no self link, but must retain the same accessible identity. Hover content shows only when the corresponding readable block/row is clipped, hidden by level, filtered or absent; selecting always permits pinning. Relation explanations can be pinned even with both endpoints visible.
- Within one row, group by target ID and show multiple role markers; in expanded detail list each source field separately. Across a frame, suppress a repeated name only when the surrounding fact already establishes the same identity **and role**. Never deduplicate on text.

**Contextual omission examples:** a full capability row in a goal's `← realizes` region omits its own `realizes: [that goal]`; a workflow inside capability focus omits that same capability link; a case row inside a step omits the owning workflow/step breadcrumb; an effect badge inside a Loan lifecycle context may omit “Loan” only if the badge cannot escape that context, including accessible description and pin behavior. Detached search/hover/pinned views restore necessary ownership. If a relation is both on a visible edge and in a compact card, show it once in the compact scene; expanded inspection can restore the field inventory. Use consumed fact IDs, not string similarity, to enforce this.

### 4.1 Effect badge and question-icon rules

Use the picked **slice of the lifecycle** motif, one badge per entity per row: `+ Loan ●→(Open)` for source-less transition creation, `Loan →(Returned)` for a move, `✎ Loan · [attribute]` for write. Several writes become `✎ Loan · N attributes`; transition plus writes adds `✎N` to the same entity badge. Target state remains intact; entity title truncates first. `tell` uses an envelope plus actor/dependency mention; delete uses its own operation icon. A generic create on an entity without lifecycle has no invented state chip. Badge pin discloses every contributing effect, exact `do/on`, transition, `from/to`, and row atomicity. Do not deduplicate the source effects from the KB, even if the viewer can combine their depiction. Agreed motif: `fable-picks.md:36–52,89–103`; ontology constraints: `K/effect.yml:2–3,11–17`; source-less transition: `K/transition.yml:3,12`.

The unused marker keeps the picked hollow red source dot and dashed empty firer slot, but says `case / choice / timeout: none` in expanded detail rather than implying only cases can fire (`fable-picks.md:73–85`; `K/transition.yml:10`). Red describes missing recorded use, not an execution failure. No final-state symbol is inferred from an empty outgoing list (`spike-lifecycles.md:7–19`; `fable-picks.md:105–115`).

Icon size is a function of **local detail and attention role**, in screen pixels: `base(level) × weight`, rounded and clamped to 10–18px. Base for L0–L4 is 14, 14, 14, 13, 12. Weight is 1 for a primary identity, .85 for a secondary relation, .75 for a decorative branch mark; selected primary may add 2px within the cap. A decision's primary glyph is 14px in a compact card and at most 16px in its inspector. A case/choice branch mark is 10–11px and may be omitted when the row kind/heading already communicates branching. Never enlarge a question mark to compensate for unreadable zoom. Hide it with the child level. Keep a 28px mouse / 44px touch interaction target independent of glyph size. These are viewer tokens; the observed gate icon is 17px and the inspector tile 30px with an 18px glyph (`lab28.tpl.html:397,468`, CSS `:67`). The proposed kind hint is only symbolic attention priority (§5).

## 5. Optional ontology hints and owner questions

**No fields are added by this spike.** Shared-hints means optional validated metadata on **kind/link definitions**, usable by both viewer and agents, with a viewer fallback. It does not authorize executable renderers, layout coordinates, concrete color values or new behavioral fields. This matches `decisions-verdict.md:23–24`. Use a versioned read-only viewer descriptor adapter now; a later separately reviewed schema change can promote accepted symbolic hints. Do not place unknown hint fields on KB entries.

| Proposed optional field | Applies to | Default if absent | Reader and constraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| `view.icon` | Kind descriptor | Viewer kind map, then neutral unknown-kind glyph | UI and agent read symbolic identity; viewer chooses SVG path. Validate symbolic name; never executable markup |
| `view.tone` | Kind descriptor | Viewer kind palette | UI and agent read a symbolic family such as motivation/data/behavior; viewer owns actual color and contrast |
| `view.attention` | Kind descriptor | `normal` | UI and agent read `quiet/normal/primary` as a salience preference, not semantic importance or review priority. Viewer maps to icon/text emphasis; context may reduce repeated decoration |
| `view.compact_fields` | Kind descriptor | Kind sentence, relevant real parts/relations, then count summary | UI and agent read ordered existing field/part paths only. No free prose templates, fabricated fields, or forced showing of empty slots. Context consumption still applies |
| `view.family` | Kind descriptor | Derived from `shape/part_of/flow/settling`, otherwise generic card | UI and agent read optional symbolic composition preference (`motivation`, `flow`, `structure`, `question`). Viewer resolves layouts. Structural metadata remains authoritative |
| `view.labels.forward` | Declared link field | Exact field name, e.g. `realizes` | UI and agent read a shared human label; validate nonempty text, always expose actual field/path in detail. Default recommendation is to keep literal names |
| `view.labels.inverse` | Declared link field | `← field`, e.g. `← realizes` | UI and agent read directional label such as “realized by” only if explicitly declared. No local SERVED BY synonym |
| `view.explanation` | Kind or declared link | Existing `about/guidance`, trimmed by UI without changing meaning | UI and agent read optional short clarification, e.g. what a synchronous call means. Text only; cannot alter `calls/covers_ends` semantics |

Versioning: descriptor carries `view_contract_version` in its transport envelope; unsupported optional keys warn and fall back, while invalid field references are rejected before use. This envelope is viewer-side until a separate schema design is approved. Export a source for every resolved label (`ontology.field`, accepted hint, or viewer command/diagnostic ID). The agent reads the same machine-readable descriptor as the UI. None of these hints is required to inspect an old snapshot.

Viewer-only: all CSS/SVG tokens, sizes and breakpoints; local levels and filters; semantic edge grouping/counts; context matrix; consumed-fact omission; canvas placement; hover/pin/open/walk actions; literal-token rendering; diagnostic/control text and overlay styles; theme/accessibility overrides. Meaningful obligations such as consistent `realizes` labels and read/tell distinction can later be captured as existing rules/decisions linked to interfaces with `satisfies`; spacing values remain assets (`decisions-verdict.md:24`; `K/interface.yml:29`). No problem/pain kind or copied reason sentence: expose existing purpose plus typed rationale/evidence separately (`decisions-verdict.md:25,34`).

Questions to file for the owner, **not decisions made here**:

1. Is literal `← realizes` readable enough, or should the link definition declare an inverse “realized by” label? Keep forward `realizes`; capability's renamed `serves` is not a reason to preserve “serves goal” (`K/capability.yml:17`).

3. Should Remind's failed Notify case reach “Every late member was reminded” with `result: success`? Both cases currently do (`graph_tool.json:1 /nodes/66,/edges/49–52`). Keep that visible as a model question.

## 7. Risks and uncertainties

- **Two lossy snapshots, not the live KB.** Claims here are limited to the files cited. `proto2.py:19–21` explains major field omissions; a fresh exporter is a prerequisite for complete decision/quantity/setting/dependency details. The task's approval is a working constraint, not evidence that a new answer is already in either snapshot. Do not overwrite answer history or remove a hedge.
- **Humanizing keys cannot infer proper names.** Prefer explicit `title` and exact `term.word` when present (`O:16`; `K/term.yml:16`); no locale/acronym dictionary is currently exported by `proto2.py:19–21`. A title override changes display, not identity or link semantics.
- **The ontology gives several graphs, not a unique zoom tree.** `realizes` may be plural for capability (`K/capability.yml:16`), interfaces can include other interfaces (`K/interface.yml:28`), calls may be recursive. Stable references, typed paths and cycle-safe summaries are necessary; initial focus choices are viewer policy. Whether the proposed 600/560/240px gates feel right needs the listed screenshots and owner reading, not an ontology debate.
- **Terminology may still feel mechanical.** `← realizes` and `covers_ends` are precise but unfamiliar. Try literal labels first with a short explanation; propose directional hints only where the reading actually fails. Do not introduce a synonym independently in one card.
- **Graph facts do not establish runtime behavior.** The walk currently records selected rows/effects, with no predicate evaluator or entity instance state (`lab28.tpl.html:490,504–518`; `spike-lifecycles.md:19,27–29`). Coverage, state roll-ups and success colors must not imply live progress, guaranteed correctness or usefulness. Condition order is authored, not computed truth (`K/case.yml:2–3`).
- **The data has a question the drawing cannot repair.** Notify's failed end continuing to Remind's success sentence, and the distinction between Mail relay as recipient and any callback actor, need owner interpretation (`graph_tool.json:1 /nodes/62,/nodes/64,/nodes/66,/edges/49–55`). Treat those as questions, not “inconsistency fixes” that change the model silently.
- **No browser implementation was built or exercised for this design.** Verification is source reading, JSON inspection, complete static call-site/word audit and document checks. Pixel thresholds and badge readability remain proposals. Existing “taken by” wording was not found in `lab28.tpl.html`; no claim about an earlier unseen version is made.
