Moving around

Scrolling (wheel or two fingers) zooms. Hold the middle button, or drag the empty background, to pan; Shift+scroll pans sideways. Click selects. You can drag a block with the left button to read under it; it springs back when you let go. Double-click a workflow to focus on it: everything unrelated disappears.
