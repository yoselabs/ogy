How to read this board

Three levels, by zoom

Map: every block is a card: icon and title, the kind word small in the corner, then what fits by priority. Flow: a workflow opens into its steps and endings. Detail: a step opens into its screen, its rows and what each row does. The same name always looks the same: icon, title, colour of its kind.

Arrows say the ontology's own word

realizes, for, actor, calls, covers ends, event, shows, and the five effects: create, write, delete, tell, transition. An arrow starts at the thing that holds the field and points at the thing it names. Hover an arrow for what the field means.

Effect badges

One badge per thing touched. A plus is a new one (green edge on the left), a pen changes fields, a bin deletes (all red), an arrow changes state, an envelope tells an actor. Fields are listed top to bottom, with the value when it is known.

Lifecycle

States are pills joined by arrows. Under the graph, each transition lists the workflows that fire it or start on it, as links. A red line says no effect fires it.

Moving around

Wheel or pinch zooms at the cursor, drag pans, click selects, Esc clears, F fits. Relations (top right) lists every relation by word; click one to see it.
