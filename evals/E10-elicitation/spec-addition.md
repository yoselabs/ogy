PROVENANCE (business kinds only)
- src: [sentence numbers] on an entry or part: the owner's words state it, at the same scope. Default mark; cheap.
- because: [ids | sentence numbers] instead of src when you widened, narrowed, combined or concluded anything: more
  states, more actors, a general rule from one example, a limit from a purpose. A generalisation is never src.
  Test: would the cited sentence alone, read aloud, make the owner say "yes, that is what I said"? If not, because.
- ?open for anything the sources do not settle (spec-v3 rule). Numbers, limits, durations, permissions, who is told
  and failure behaviour are open unless a sentence gives them.
- Parts inherit the entry's mark; mark a part only when it differs. Never mark technical kinds.
ELICITATION
- decision {question, settles: [slot addresses], ?options: {key: {label, sets: {slot: value}}}, ?default: key}.
  Two shapes: a choice (options, every option sets the same slots) or a fill-in (no options: the owner gives the
  values, e.g. one "price list" question settles every money amount at once).
- An open slot points at its decision: ?open: {decision: decision/<id>}. Group slots into one decision when one owner
  sentence would settle all of them (same subject or same policy: amounts, on failure, how long, who may, who is told).
- Rank decisions by: money moved, external told, irreversible end, permission > everything else; then by the number
  of slots settled. Before asking, derive anything entailed, technical, or already covered by a stated rule.
