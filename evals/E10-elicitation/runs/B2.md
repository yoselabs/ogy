## Owner questions

1. Prices (decision/prices). What are these four amounts? The yearly fee for Basic, the yearly fee for Plus, the deposit for a power tool (one amount for every power tool, or does it depend on the tool?), and the late fee per day.
2. When a tool comes back damaged, or is lost, who pays what (decision/damage_money)? My proposal: Marta decides the repair charge case by case, the deposit is held until she does and then applied (any rest goes back), and a lost tool is charged the tool's replacement value, which the volunteer writes down when the donated tool is added. Or: damaged means the deposit is simply kept and nothing more. Which?
3. When Stripe cannot take a payment (decision/payment_failure): joining with no membership, a yearly renewal failing, a deposit failing at the counter. My proposal: a failed join gives no membership; a failed renewal emails the member and gives them a grace period (say two weeks) before the membership ends and they cannot borrow; a failed deposit means the loan does not start. Right, and how long is the grace period?
4. Who may do what (decision/who_may)? My proposal: any volunteer can take returns, start loans, inspect donations and mark the safety intro; only Marta decides what a member pays for a repair. Is that right, or should only Marta mark the safety intro?
5. Upgrading from Basic to Plus (decision/upgrade_price): does the member pay just the difference for the rest of the year (renewal date stays), or the full Plus fee with a fresh year starting today?
6. Blocking (decision/late_block): after how many days late is a member blocked, and does the late fee count from the first day late?
7. Waitlist (decision/waitlist_claim): when a tool comes back and the next person is emailed, how long do they have to claim it? After that, does it go to the next person on the list?

## Assumed for read-back

Each is in force until the owner changes it.

1. Opening hours: Tuesday and Thursday 17:00-20:00, Saturday 09:00-12:00. (set/opening_slot)
2. Tool categories: drills, saws, ladders, cleaning, garden, other; volunteers pick one, cannot add new ones.
3. A donor does not have to be a member; a donated tool that is not good enough is simply not added and the donor is not emailed.
4. "Out at once" counts reserved tools as well as tools on loan.
5. A member may cancel a reservation before pickup; a reservation not picked up on its day is cancelled and the tool goes back on the shelf (or to the waitlist).
6. At pickup the counter app rechecks the rules (plan, safety intro, age, blocked, limit); loans start only on the reserved day.
7. A lost tool that turns up again goes back to available; a tool is retired only after Marta has looked at it; late fee is charged when the tool is returned; if unpaid the tool is back but the member stays blocked until they pay.
8. When a blocked member returns and pays, they are unblocked at once unless another tool of theirs is late.
9. A blocked member keeps existing reservations but cannot make new ones. A member with a lost tool and an unpaid replacement stays blocked.
10. Waitlist: first come first served; a member can leave it; one can join while the tool is on loan, reserved or in repair, not when retired. A tool offered to a waitlisted member is held for them (shown as reserved) until they claim it or the time runs out.
11. Claiming a waitlist offer means making a normal reservation for a pickup day; all the usual rules apply.
12. Downgrading Plus to Basic is not offered. Members under eighteen may join and borrow hand tools.
13. Rating is optional; a member who does not rate is not asked again. Ratings are only stored for now; nothing shows them (the board report does not use them).
14. The reminder email goes at 09:00 the day before the due date; the late, block and lost checks run daily at 06:00.
15. The monthly report is made on the 1st for the month just ended and emailed to the board through Postmark. "Money we took in" is the total of three parts: memberships (new, renewals, upgrades), fees (late fees, repair charges, replacement charges), and deposits kept. Most borrowed tools lists the top 10. "Late loans" counts loans returned late plus those still out past due.
16. All emails go through Postmark (external/postmark); all payments, deposit charges and refunds through Stripe (external/stripe).
17. The Google Sheet of volunteer shifts stays outside the system; the app only records who opened the counter at the start of a shift, never when it ends, and does not stop anyone using the counter. Sam (coordinator) is not modelled.
18. Safety intro sessions (once a month, run by Marta) are not scheduled by the system; only the mark on the profile matters.
19. The yearly renewal is charged on the anniversary of joining; the first year counts from the day the fee is paid.
20. The deposit is refunded on Stripe immediately when a power tool is returned fine.
21. "Dead easy" counter: a new volunteer can finish a pickup and a return unaided, in at most three taps each.
22. "Lost after a month" means 1 calendar month after the due date.
23. Member card: each member has a card id; the member profile (name, birth date, plan, card, safety intro done) is the only member data kept. About 400 members is context only, not a limit.
