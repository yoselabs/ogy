## Owner questions

1. What are the money amounts: the yearly fee for basic and for plus, the deposit for a power tool, the late fee per day, and how the replacement charge for a lost tool is worked out (a fixed amount or the tool's own price)?
2. When a tool comes back damaged, who decides whether the member pays for the repair (Marta?), how is the amount set, and does the deposit stay with the library in that case?
3. If a yearly payment fails, what should happen to the member (a grace period, then what?), and what does an upgrade from basic to plus cost midyear (the full plus fee, or the difference)?
4. How many days late before a member is blocked, and do late fees stop growing once the tool counts as lost?
5. How do you know a member is eighteen (birth date typed in the app, or ID shown at the counter), and who is allowed to mark the safety intro as done (only Marta, or any volunteer)?
6. When a tool comes back and the next person on the waitlist gets the email, how long do they have to claim it before it goes to the next one?
7. If someone reserves a tool and does not show up on the pickup day, what happens to the reservation (it lapses the same day, or it is held longer)?

## Assumed for read-back

Money and numbers (in force until answered; amounts are placeholders, no basis in your words):
- Q1: basic 30, plus 60, deposit 50, late fee 2 per day, replacement = the tool's own replacement value, which the volunteer enters when cataloguing.
- Q2: Marta decides per case; member pays actual repair cost; deposit refunded unless she charges; a failed repair payment does not block anything.
- Q3: retry for 7 days then membership ends; upgrade costs the plus fee minus the basic fee, no proration. A blocked member's renewal still runs.
- Q4: blocked after 14 days late; late fees stop when the tool is marked lost.
- Q5: birth date entered at joining and checked at the counter by sight; only Marta and the volunteers on shift may mark the intro.
- Q6: 2 days; if it runs out or they decline, the next person is emailed; if nobody is left the tool becomes available.
- Q7: reservation lapses at the end of the pickup day and the tool becomes available again.

Everything else:
- "A month after it was due" for lost means one calendar month.
- The nightly sweep runs at 03:00 and does reminders, late days and lost checks.
- The tools-out limit counts reserved plus active loans; a reservation that would go over is refused.
- A lost tool that turns up returns to available; the replacement charge stays unless the board refunds it.
- If the deposit charge fails, no loan starts. If a late member cannot pay at the counter, the return is not accepted and the block stays.
- If the replacement charge fails, the member stays blocked until paid.
- A failed waitlist email skips to the next person. Waitlist offers also fire after repair, not only after a fine return.
- The rating is optional. Ratings are stored per member and tool; nothing shows them yet (you did not say how they are used).
- Donated tools that are not good go back to the donor. Anyone may donate, member or not.
- Volunteers type their name at shift start; no accounts. Shift sign-up stays in Sam's Google Sheet, outside the app.
- Tool categories are a free list volunteers extend.
- Board report: top 10 most borrowed tools, sent by email through Postmark on the first of each month.
- Counter app "dead easy" means at most 3 taps from scan to started loan.
- Members get emails for reminders, blocks, waitlist offers and lost-tool charges; no other messages.
- Stripe and Postmark versions and the two app screens are technical and left to the agent to propose.
- Out of scope (not captured because you did not ask for it): volunteer shift scheduling, member-facing history pages, QR sticker printing.
