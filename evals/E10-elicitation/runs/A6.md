## Assumed for read-back

Each item is in force until the owner corrects it; the matching `?open` carries it in the entries where one exists.

- Fee, deposit, late fee amounts (if Q1 gets only partial answers): basic 20, plus 40, deposit 50 for every power tool, late fee 1 per day capped at the replacement cost.
- "A while" to claim: 48 hours. "Too long late": 7 days. (Fallbacks for Q3 and Q2.)
- Under 18: age is worked out from a birthdate the member enters when joining; we do not check ID at the counter. The system refuses power tools for under 18s in the app and on the counter screen.
- Hand tools: basic and plus members may borrow them; only plus members may borrow power tools (the owner said so for plus; basic power-tool access is refused).
- A "month" for lost is 30 days after the due date. The replacement charge is the tool's replacement cost; the system needs a replacement cost per tool: assumed the volunteer enters it when adding the tool to the catalog (adds an attribute, `replacement_cost`, to entity/tool).
- A lost tool's deposit is kept toward the replacement charge.
- Block lifts automatically when the overdue tool is back and all fees are paid (not a volunteer action).
- Reservation limit: tools out plus open reservations may not exceed the plan's maximum.
- A member may reserve only on days listed in the opening slots; reservation can be made any time up to the pickup day (no limit on how far ahead; assumed up to 14 days).
- One reservation per tool; the waitlist is first come, first served; a member can be on several waitlists and can leave one.
- A waitlisted member who claims within the window gets a reservation for the next pickup day (same rules as a normal reservation, including limits).
- The due date does not move if the pickup day is skipped (loan starts at the scan, due = pickup day + loan length, as the owner said).
- Late days count calendar days, including days the shed is closed.
- The reminder email goes at 06:00 the day before the due date; the lateness check runs daily at 06:00.
- Returns after the due date still go through the normal inspection; the late fee is charged at the return, paid on Stripe or on a later bill, and until it is paid the member stays blocked if blocked.
- Fees, deposits and charges are all taken on Stripe. Late fees and repair charges that are not paid at the counter are collected through an app payment link; the app does not hold a balance.
- The rating is optional, once per loan, only after a returned tool, and is only shown to the member (no public tool reviews) unless the owner says otherwise.
- Marta's repair decision (free, charge, retire) is the only damage decision; the volunteer only says fine or damaged.
- Volunteers need an account or access to the counter app; assumed the counter app is opened with a shared volunteer login plus entering their own name, which is what gets recorded as who is on shift. Shift sign-ups in the Google Sheet stay outside the system and are not read.
- A shift record is just who and when; it is not used to block or allow anything.
- Safety intro: marked by Marta (or a volunteer on her behalf) on the profile and never expires.
- Donations: the volunteer adds name, category, photo and power-tool flag; categories are a list the library manages (assumed: hammer/hand, garden, power, cleaning, ladders, other, editable by Sam).
- Printing the QR sticker is done from the counter app; one sticker per tool, stays for the tool's life.
- Retired tools stay in history and are never offered again.
- Monthly report: previous calendar month; "most borrowed" is the top 10 tools by loan count; "late loans" counts loans returned or lost after their due date; money in is split into membership fees, deposits kept, late fees, repair and replacement charges. Emailed to the board by Postmark.
- Membership: 400 members, no cap on the number of members. A member leaving is not handled in the system (assumed: the member simply does not renew).
- Email is the only notification channel; no SMS or push.
- Everything runs on the app (members, phone and web) and the counter app (tablet at the shed); there is no separate admin tool beyond the fixer screen and the report.
- Ease at the counter: one decision per screen, big buttons, plain words, scanning is the main action; this becomes quality/counter_easy and is checked with a first-time volunteer.
- Upgrades take effect immediately for new loans; downgrades are not offered.
