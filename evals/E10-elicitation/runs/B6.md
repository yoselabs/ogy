## Owner questions

1. Prices: what is the yearly fee for basic and for plus, what does an upgrade from basic to plus cost, how much is the deposit for a power tool, and what is the late fee per day? (price_list)
2. When a tool comes back damaged, who decides whether the member pays for the repair, and when do you keep the deposit? And what do you charge for a lost tool: the tool's replacement price, a fixed amount? (damage_money)
3. If a member's yearly renewal payment fails, what should happen: lapse at once, a short grace period first, or stay a member but no new loans? (renewal_failure)
4. How many days late before someone is blocked, is there a ceiling on late fees, and do they pay the late fee at the counter when they return the tool? (lateness)
5. When a tool comes back and someone is waiting, how long do they have to claim it, and should the tool be held for them in the meantime? (waitlist_window)
6. Besides the day-before reminder and the waitlist email, should members get an email when they are blocked, charged a late fee, charged for a repair, or told a tool is lost? (notifications)
7. If someone reserves a tool and does not turn up on the pickup day, should the reservation end that day, wait one more open day, or stay until they cancel? (pickup_noshow)

## Assumed for read-back

Each is in force until the owner corrects it. Entries above carry the same assumption in their ?open.

1. The board is the owner for questions; the monthly report goes by email through Postmark on the 1st for the month before; top 5 most borrowed tools.
2. Age is checked from a date of birth the member gives when joining; the system applies the under-18 rule.
3. Marta or any volunteer may mark the safety intro on a profile.
4. Any volunteer at the counter may start loans, take returns, accept donations and release deposits; Marta alone decides repairs and retiring.
5. Plan limits count tools on loan only, not reservations.
6. Upgrade charges the difference between the two fees at once through Stripe; the renewal date stays the same. No downgrade or cancellation is offered in the system.
7. Reservations are for one tool and one pickup day; a member can cancel their own reservation.
8. Waitlist is first come, first served; a member can be on a tool's waitlist once; if a waiter does not claim in time they drop off and the next is offered.
9. Rating is asked after every return (damaged too) and can be skipped; ratings are stored on the tool and not shown to other members.
10. Late days are calendar days counted after the due date; the late fee is the number of late days times the daily fee, up to a ceiling only if you name one.
11. A lost tool stays lost; the member keeps being blocked until the replacement is paid (the block rule treats late loans; lost loans end the loan and free the member's count only after payment). Open if you want something different.
12. A tool that fails the donation inspection is handed back or discarded and not recorded.
13. A tool's category is free text typed by the volunteer; photo is one picture.
14. Shift sign-up stays in Sam's Google Sheet outside the system; the counter app only records who opens it, and shows that name on loans and returns. A shift has no end time.
15. "Late loans" in the report counts loans that started in the month and were returned late or are still overdue; "fees" means late fees, repair charges and replacement charges; "memberships" means joining, renewal and upgrade payments.
16. The daily checks (reminder, late fee, block, lost) run at 03:00 each day over every loan on loan.
17. The counter app is "easy enough" when a new volunteer starts a loan in under two minutes after one demo.
18. Deposit declined at pickup: the loan does not start and the tool stays reserved to that pickup day.
19. Spec gaps noted for the tool, not for the owner: no way to say "before due" in a trigger (reminder uses a daily schedule and a date test), schedule workflows run once per matching row, and "first waiter" needs an ordered list expression.
