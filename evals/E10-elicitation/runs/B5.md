4. If a member's yearly renewal payment fails, do they lose access straight away or get a grace period, and should we email them?

6. When a tool comes back and the next person on the waitlist is emailed, how long do they have to claim it, and is the tool held for them meanwhile?

## Assumed for read-back

- Currency is whatever the Stripe account uses; one currency only.
- Opening hours: Tuesday and Thursday evening 17:00-20:00, Saturday morning 09:00-12:00. Pickup date must be one of these days.
- Date of birth is collected at joining to enforce the under-18 power-tool rule. Under-18s may join and borrow hand tools on basic or plus.
- A member can pick a pickup day up to 14 days ahead. A reservation counts toward the tools-out limit only once the loan starts; the reserve step also refuses when current loans plus open reservations reach the limit.
- A member can cancel a reservation any time before the pickup day; the tool goes back to available.
- Loans cannot be extended or renewed.
- Reservation and pickup guards (blocked, plan, safety intro, age, limit) are checked at both reserve and checkout.
- The volunteer scanning a member card must match the member on the reservation. No borrowing on the spot without a reservation.
- The deposit is charged on Stripe at pickup and refunded to the card on a fine return. A declined deposit refuses the loan and leaves the reservation held until the shed closes.
- The deposit is one flat amount for every power tool.
- Late fee accrues daily on the loan from the day after the due date and is collected at return on Stripe at the counter. The block lifts automatically when the tool is back and fees are paid. A blocked member's current reservations stay.
- The nightly check runs at 06:00. One reminder email, to the member only, sent the day before due.
- A lost tool is charged the replacement charge (per-tool value set at cataloguing) on the saved Stripe card; if the charge fails the member stays blocked and owes it. A lost tool can later be marked found by a volunteer and goes back to available.
- A tool in repair or lost cannot be reserved (entailed by the tool lifecycle: reserve only from available).
- Waitlist: one entry per member per tool, first come first served, a member can leave. When the tool returns, the tool is held for the offered member until the claim window ends, then the offer passes to the next person.
- Only a member in good standing (active) can join a waitlist or reserve.
- Safety intro is marked once by Marta and never expires. Marta runs it monthly outside the system.
- A repair that the fixer finishes closes the loan (returned) and frees the member's tools-out slot; a tool beyond fixing is retired. Retired tools disappear from the catalog.
- Donation not good enough goes back to the donor with no record. A good donation is entered by the volunteer with name, category (free text), photo, power flag; the app generates the QR code for a sticker; replacement cost is entered at the same time.
- Ratings are optional, one per loan, asked in the member app right after the return is recorded; the average is shown on the tool's catalog page.
- The counter app opens a shift when a volunteer logs in and starts it; there is no shift end, and the app uses the latest open shift. Sam's Google Sheet stays outside the system. Starting a shift is not required to use the counter.
- Volunteers have individual logins in the counter app (needed to record who is on).
- Monthly report: produced on the 1st for the previous month and emailed to the board through Postmark. It lists the top 5 most borrowed tools, the number of loans that were late in the month, and money taken by kind: fees (late, repair and replacement charges), deposits kept, memberships (including upgrades).
- Renewal is automatic on the saved Stripe card. Joining stores the card for renewal and later charges.
- No downgrade, cancellation or refund of membership fees is offered.
- "A month after it was due" means the same day in the next calendar month.
- "Dead easy" counter app: a new volunteer completes a checkout in under 2 minutes with no help (see quality/counter-ease).
- Emails and screens are in one language only; the counter needs network access.
