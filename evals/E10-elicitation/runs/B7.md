## Assumed for read-back

Confirm in one batch; each is already in the entries as `open.assumed`.

- Fees if you skip Q1: basic 30 a year, plus 50, power-tool deposit 50, late fee 1 a day (Stripe account currency).
- A blocked member can still return tools and pay; blocked until the tool is back and fees are paid (your words); we unblock automatically on payment.
- Lost: when the tool is a month past its due date, the replacement charge is taken; a tool that is found later goes back to the shelf and the charge is not refunded.
- Late fee is charged per full day late, counted from the due date; the one-month lost rule overrides blocking.
- Deposit: charged on Stripe at pickup, released when a fine return is logged; kept (or used for repair) when damaged or lost.
- A power-tool check (plus plan, safety intro, 18 or over) is applied both when reserving and at the counter at pickup.
- Members under 18 can join basic and borrow hand tools. Date of birth is asked when joining and is not verified.
- Limit of tools out counts only tools on loan, not reservations. A reservation can only be for one of the shed's open days.
- Reserving ahead: up to 2 weeks (assumed); one active reservation per tool; member can cancel until the pickup day.
- No-show: the hold ends at the end of the pickup day and the tool becomes available again (and the waitlist is offered it).
- Waitlist is first come, first served; when the claim window ends the offer passes to the next person; one claim window per person per return.
- Renewal: automatic yearly charge; on failure the member is emailed, has 7 days, then lapses and cannot borrow; paying revives them.
- Upgrade: pay the difference of the two yearly fees, pro-rated by days left; no downgrade or refund path (not mentioned).
- Reminder email goes out the day before due; late and lost notices are also emailed through Postmark; the member is told when blocked.
- Marta's decisions (repair, retire) are made in the counter app by her; the repair charge is capped at the deposit for power tools.
- Safety intro is for life once marked (no expiry). Marta or any volunteer may mark it per Q5.
- Ratings: optional, one per loan, 1 to 5; shown on the tool in the catalog; skippable.
- Declined donations are not recorded. Category list starts with your examples and volunteers add more.
- Volunteer on shift: picked from a list in the counter app; the Google Sheet and Sam's rota stay outside the app and are not read.
- Board report: sent by email on the 1st of each month for the month before; "fees" means late fees, "memberships" yearly fees.
- "Late loans" in the report means loans returned after the due date, or still out past it, in that month.
- Counter app: a first-time volunteer starts a loan in about 3 minutes with no help; no other usability target.
- No requirement for scale beyond about 400 members; no refunds, no member self-service for cancelling the plan, no multi-shed support.
- Opening hours are the three days only; the app does not enforce clock times within a day.
