## Assumed for read-back

- Basic yearly fee 30; plus 60 (placeholders, owner to set; covered by Q1 in part: amounts).
- Late fee 1 per day, no cap except the replacement cost (Q1 covers amount; cap assumed).
- Block after 7 days late (Q2 asks; assumed otherwise).
- Claim window 48 hours; tool held for the offered member (Q3 asks).
- Damage charge set by fixer; deposit applied first, rest on Stripe (Q4 asks).
- Reservation lapses at end of pickup day; counts toward tools-out limit (Q5 asks).
- Upgrade pays the fee difference; renewal date unchanged (Q6 asks).
- Failed renewal: no retry, member lapsed, cannot reserve, may return (Q7 asks).
- Age is checked against a birth date given at join, for power tools only; age under 18 never gets power tools even on plus.
- The safety intro is marked by the fixer (Marta) and never expires.
- Members can reserve only a tool that is available; reserving a tool does not need payment unless it is a power tool (deposit at pickup).
- Loan length for plus members applies to hand tools too (plan decides length, not tool type).
- Basic members borrow only hand tools; "hand tool" means is_power = false.
- Waitlist is first come first served; a member may leave a waitlist at any time.
- The pickup day must be a future opening day; the system never schedules pickups on closed days. Opening hours themselves (evenings, morning times) are not enforced, only the three days.
- Returns are accepted only at the counter during opening hours; the app cannot return a tool.
- Lost tool: member charged replacement cost; if later found, tool returns to available and the charge is not refunded unless the owner says so.
- The coordinator's Google Sheet is not integrated; the counter app only records who opened it.
- Rating is optional and one per loan; ratings are stored but not used elsewhere yet.
- A lapsed or blocked member cannot reserve or join the waitlist.
- Report is emailed to the board on the last day of each month at 18:00; "money" lists fees, deposits kept (applied to damage or loss), and memberships separately.
- Emails: reminder at the daily 06:00 run for loans due the next day; all through Postmark.
- Retiring a tool is the fixer's call (when unfixable); a volunteer may also retire a lost tool after the replacement is charged.
- Counter app quality: pickup and return each need at most 3 taps/scans and no typing.
- Safety intro is monthly and run by the fixer; attendance is marked by the fixer only, not by volunteers.
- Deposit is one amount for all power tools and is released at return if the tool is fine.
