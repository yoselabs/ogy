## Assumed for read-back

Each item is one `?open` with the proposed answer in force until confirmed.

- Members under 18 may borrow hand tools on either plan; only power tools are barred. (You said no power tools under eighteen; nothing about hand tools.)
- Age is checked from a birthdate given at joining; volunteers do not check ID. 
- Hand tools need no deposit; the deposit applies to power tools only.
- Plus members' longer loans apply to hand tools too (loan length is by plan, not by tool).
- Reserved tools count toward the three or five at once; so do tools on loan.
- The safety intro stays valid for life once marked, and is not renewed.
- A basic member cannot reserve a power tool at all; the app does not show the button.
- Blocked members may still return tools and pay at the counter.
- A tool that is later found after being marked lost goes back to available; the replacement charge is not refunded unless the volunteer says so.
- The late fee keeps counting each day until the tool is back or declared lost; it stops at lost.
- The deposit is kept (not released) when a tool is declared lost.
- Late fees and the replacement charge go to the member's card on file at Stripe; if that fails the member stays blocked.
- The reminder email is sent at 09:00 the day before the due date; due dates that fall on a closed day stay as they are (due date is pickup day plus loan length, as you said).
- Waitlist: a member may be on a waitlist for several tools; one person may not be offered two tools at once beyond their limit.
- A member who leaves the waitlist can do so any time in the app.
- Rating is skippable, whole stars 1 to 5, and ratings are not shown to other members.
- A declined donation goes back to the donor.
- Every volunteer may inspect donations and run returns; the counter app logs which volunteer did each action from the shift record.
- Sam's Google Sheet of shifts stays outside the system; the app only records who opens the counter.
- Tool categories are free text typed by the volunteer; there is no fixed category list.
- The monthly report covers the previous calendar month, is emailed to the board on the first of the month, shows the top 10 most borrowed tools, the number of late loans, and money taken split by memberships, deposits kept, late fees, repair charges, replacement charges.
- Emails go only through Postmark; a failed email is retried once and then shown to a volunteer at the counter.
- "Dead easy" counter means a first-time volunteer starts a loan from scan to done within 2 minutes with no help.

- Cancelling a membership is not in scope for now; nobody can quit through the app.
