```yaml
# docs/kb/product/library/term.yml  (list file)
- {id: loan, means: A tool in a member's hands from the pickup day until the counter takes it back.}
- {id: reservation, means: A member's claim on a tool for one open shed day, made in the app.}
- {id: waitlist, means: The ordered list of members waiting for a tool that is out.}
- {id: power-tool, means: A tool marked as a power tool in the catalog; needs plus, a deposit, the safety intro and age 18+.}
- {id: safety-intro, means: The monthly session Marta runs; passing it is marked on the member profile.}
- {id: deposit, means: Money charged on Stripe when a power-tool loan starts, released on a fine return.}
- {id: late-fee, means: A charge for every day a loan is past its due date.}
- {id: block, means: A member cannot borrow anything until the overdue tool is back and fees are paid.}
- {id: lost, means: A tool not back a month after its due date; the member pays the replacement.}
- {id: counter, means: The shed desk where pickups and returns happen; also the volunteer's counter app.}
```
