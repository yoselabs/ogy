## Assumed for read-back

All below are in force until the owner answers; confirm or correct in one batch.

1. Upgrade basic to plus: plus fee minus basic fee, paid at once, takes effect immediately, renewal date unchanged. Downgrade/cancel: not in this version.
2. "A month" for lost = 30 days after the due date. Lost tool that later returns goes back to available; the replacement charge stays paid.
3. Blocked member is unblocked as soon as the tool is back and all fees are paid; nothing else.
4. A blocked or lapsed member cannot reserve; loans already out continue.
5. A lapsed member (failed renewal) can renew later and becomes active again.
6. Reminder is sent once, the day before the due date, by Postmark; late-fee checks run once a day.
7. Late fee has no cap.
8. Under-18 people can be members on hand tools; birth date is asked at joining. Power tools need plus plan + safety intro done + age 18 or older (all three).
9. Deposit applies to power tools only, charged at pickup on Stripe; if declined the loan does not start and the tool stays reserved that day. Deposit released when the tool returns fine; kept against repair/lost charges.
10. Rating is asked after every return (fine or damaged), optional, 1 to 5; the tool shows an average.
11. Tool replacement cost is entered on the tool by the volunteer at donation time (not set per category).
12. A declined donation is not recorded.
13. Pickup day must be one of the three opening days; no cap on how far ahead one can reserve (proposed: up to the next opening day after two weeks).
14. Waitlist is first come first served; an expired offer goes to the next person; leaving the waitlist is allowed any time before an offer.
15. The Google Sheet stays Sam's; the system does not read it. The counter app only records which member opened the counter, assuming volunteers are also members with a card.
16. Counter app refusals show a plain reason and cannot be overridden by the volunteer.
17. Retiring a tool: Marta (fixer) or a volunteer marks it retired from in-repair, lost or available; retired is final.
18. Monthly board report goes out on the 1st, covering: most borrowed tools (by loan count), number of late loans, money in split by fees, deposits kept, memberships. Who receives it: the board persona, by email.
19. Safety intro: Marta marks it on the profile after the monthly session; it does not expire.
20. Renewal is an automatic yearly Stripe charge on each member's join anniversary.
21. "Dead easy" counter: at most three screens per pickup and per return (proposed target, not agreed).
22. Hand tools vs power tools: only two categories needed for rules; other category names are free-form labels.
