## Assumed for read-back

Confirm in one batch. Each is already in the entries as an assumed value with an open mark.

- Fees: Basic 30 a year, Plus 60, power deposit 50 flat, late fee 1 per day, upgrade pays the difference. (Q1 will replace these.)
- A failed deposit payment at the counter means no loan starts.
- Late fee is worked out on return (days late times the daily fee) and is not capped.
- A blocked member is unblocked automatically once the late tool is back and every due charge is paid. Payment is made in the app by Stripe, not at the counter.
- A member under 18 may still join, on Basic, and borrow hand tools.
- Reservations are checked for power-tool eligibility at reserve time and again at the counter. The three/five tool limit counts tools on loan, not reservations.
- A member can only collect on the reserved pickup day, and the due date is that day plus the loan length.
- The pickup day may be chosen up to 14 days ahead, among the opening slots (Tue evening, Thu evening, Sat morning).
- When a waitlisted tool is offered it counts as reserved for that person. If nobody claims, it goes to the next in line. A tool back from repair also triggers the waitlist.
- A lost tool that turns up later can be put back to available.
- Rating is optional, and applies to damaged returns too.
- A donation that is not good enough is handed back or recycled by the volunteer, with nothing recorded.
- Safety intro never expires; Marta's monthly session itself is outside the system.
- Reminders and the late check run daily at 08:00. Monthly report goes out on the 1st at 09:00, with the 10 most borrowed tools.
- "Fees" in the report means late, repair and replacement charges. Deposits kept and memberships are listed separately.
- "Late loans" counts loans returned after the due date plus lost ones.
- Shift sign-up stays in Sam's Google Sheet, outside the app. The counter app only records who is on when a volunteer opens it, with no end time.
- Counter "dead easy": a first-time volunteer starts a loan in under 2 minutes with no help.
- A renewal that comes due while a member is blocked is charged, and the member stays blocked.
- Scale of about 400 members is context only and sets no requirement.
- Out of scope: how members pay for charges inside the shed, pause of memberships, quitting and refunds, and tool categories beyond a free-text name.
