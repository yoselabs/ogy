## Owner questions

1. What are the money amounts: yearly fee for basic and for plus, the deposit for a power tool (flat or per tool), and the late fee per day?
2. When does "late for too long" block a member, and what lifts the block (tool back, fees paid, both)? And when a tool is lost, do you keep the deposit as well as charging the replacement?
3. How long does a waitlisted person have to claim a tool, and if they miss it, does it go to the next person?
4. When a tool comes back damaged, who decides what the member pays for the repair and how much, and is the deposit used for it first?
5. What happens when a yearly renewal payment fails: does the member lose access right away, get some grace, and what about tools they still have out?
6. If someone reserves a tool and does not turn up on the pickup day, what happens to the reservation, and can they reserve again?
7. When someone upgrades from basic to plus mid-year, do they pay the difference now or at renewal, and can they ever go back down?

## Assumed for read-back

1. Prices: basic 30 per year, plus 60 per year, deposit flat 40 for any power tool, late fee 1 per day. (Q1 fallback.)
2. Block after 14 days late; block lifts only when all overdue tools are back and nothing is owed. (Q2 fallback.)
3. Lost = 30 days after due date (your words: "a month"); replacement price is set per tool when added to the catalog; deposit kept on loss.
4. Waitlist offer lapses after 48 hours; lapsed person is not re-queued. (Q3 fallback.)
5. Fixer decides per case if and how much the member pays; deposit applied first. Hand tools damaged: same decision, no deposit.
