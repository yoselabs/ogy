## Owner questions

1. A tool comes back and someone is waiting: how long do they have to claim it before it goes to the next person?
2. How much is the late fee per day, and after how many late days do you block the member?
3. What do the two plans cost per year, and how big is the deposit on a power tool?
4. If someone reserves a tool and never turns up on the pickup day, what should happen to the reservation?
5. When a tool is damaged, who decides how much the member pays for the repair (Marta?), and does it come out of the deposit?
6. If the yearly renewal on Stripe fails, what can the member still do?
7. Do you know a member's age already (birth date at joining), or should the app ask for it?

## Assumed for read-back

- Fees: basic 20, plus 40 per year. Deposit 30 per power tool. (Q3 backup.)
- Late fee 1 per day; blocked from day 7 late; unblocked when the tool is returned and fees paid. Lost at 30 days late; replacement charge is each tool's recorded replacement value.
- Claim window 3 days; if not claimed the next waiting member is offered it; waitlist order is first come first served.
- A reservation not picked up expires at end of the pickup day; tool released and the waitlist runs.
- Renewal fail: member can still return tools but cannot reserve; lapses after 14 days.
- Upgrade: pay the full difference between fees now; renewal date does not change. Downgrade not supported.
- Repair share is recorded by Marta as none, partial or full; deposit is kept up to that share, the rest released. Amount of a partial share is typed in by Marta.
- Birth date is asked when joining; under-18 members can still join and borrow hand tools.
- A member can reserve only on an opening day, and only one reservation per tool.
- Reservation counts toward the loan limit (at-limit counts open reservations as well as tools out). To confirm.
- The reminder is sent the morning before the due date; the late job runs once a day.
- Safety intro is marked by Marta (or a volunteer at her request) on the member's profile; it does not expire.
- Ratings: average stars shown on the tool in the catalog; no one else uses them.
- Donated tools get a QR sticker when added; replacement value is entered by the volunteer at that time.
- The shift Google Sheet stays Sam's and outside the app; the app records only who opened the counter (volunteer name picked from a list typed in or chosen at open).
- Report: emailed monthly to the board on the 1st; "most borrowed" = top 10 tools by loans in the month; "late loans" = loans that closed or went overdue in the month; money = fees, deposits kept, memberships, split by type.
- Emails: all via Postmark; one language; no SMS.
- Retired tools stay in history but disappear from the catalog.
- "Dead easy counter" is taken as: pickup is two scans plus one confirm; return is one scan plus fine/damaged.
- Opening hours (Tuesday and Thursday evening, Saturday morning) are fixed; exact clock times are not needed by the app, only the day.
- Memberships are per person, no family accounts; one card per member.
