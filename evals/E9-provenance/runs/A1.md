## Questions for the owner

1. What are the money amounts: yearly fee for basic and for plus, the deposit (flat or per tool), the late fee per day, and the replacement charge (per tool)? None were said, and the report and the code both need them.
2. How many days late before a member is blocked from borrowing, and exactly how does the block end ("until you bring it back and pay": the moment both are done, or does a volunteer decide)?
3. How long is "a while" for a waitlisted person to claim a tool, and while an offer is open, is the tool held back from everyone else, including walk-ups at the counter?
4. When a tool comes back damaged, who decides if the member pays for the repair and how much, and what happens to the deposit: kept, partly kept, set against the repair?
5. If a member does not turn up on the reserved day, what happens to the reservation: does the tool stay held, is it freed after the day, or can they collect any later day?
6. Does the three-or-five-tools limit count tools on loan only, or reserved ones too, and does a member's age get checked from a birth date on the profile or with an ID at the counter?
7. When the yearly renewal payment fails, what happens: a grace period, a block, or lost membership? And what does upgrading mid-year cost: full plus fee, the difference, or pro-rated?
8. Who marks the safety intro on a profile (Marta or any volunteer), does it ever expire, and do members who attended on paper need it entered afterwards?
9. If a lost tool turns up, or a tool in repair cannot be reached for a long time, what should happen: can lost tools return to the shelf, can a tool be retired from any state?
10. Which exact opening and closing times do the three slots have, and does the shed ever close (holidays), which affects what pickup days exist?
11. For the board report: how many "most borrowed" tools, does "late loans" mean returned late or currently late, is "money we took in" all five kinds (membership, upgrades, late fees, kept deposits, repair and replacement charges), and which day of the month must it arrive and in what form?
12. What happens to a donation that is not good enough (give back, recycle), and who generates and prints the QR sticker?
13. Do ratings only exist for the member's feedback, or are they shown in the catalog or used to decide repairs and retirements?
14. Does Sam need anything from the system (should the app read the Google Sheet, or are shifts only recorded when a volunteer opens the counter app)? What should the system do when no volunteer has opened the counter on a shift day?
15. What is the tool category list, and are there any members who must be treated differently (children, families, concessions)?
16. How will we know the counter app is "dead easy": for instance, can a first-time volunteer finish a loan unaided, and in how long?
