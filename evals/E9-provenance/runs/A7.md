## Questions for the owner

1. Money when things go wrong: if a tool comes back damaged, is the deposit kept, partly kept or released, and how does the member pay for a repair (from the deposit or a new charge)? You said "deposits kept" belongs in the board report, so some are kept, but never said when.
2. Lost tools: what is the replacement price (the tool's own value, a flat amount, set by whom)? Is it charged to the saved card, is the deposit taken against it, and do daily late fees stop once the tool is lost?
3. How late is "too long" before a member is blocked, and what does "pay" mean to lift the block (late fees only, or also anything else)? Is the block lifted automatically when the tool is back and paid, or by a volunteer?
4. What are the amounts: yearly fee for basic and plus, power-tool deposit (one amount or per tool), late fee per day (any cap)?
5. Upgrading from basic to plus: what does a member pay mid-year, and does the renewal date move? What happens when automatic renewal fails: does the member lapse, can they keep tools they hold, are they told?
6. Is "a month" for a lost tool exactly 30 days after due, or the same date next month?
7. How long is "a while" for the next waitlisted person to claim a tool, and does claiming also choose a pickup day? What happens to the tool while they decide?
8. Reservations: what happens if the member does not show up on the pickup day? Can a loan start on a different day than reserved? Does "three at once" count reserved tools or only tools on loan?
9. Can an under-18 person be a member at all (hand tools only)? How is age known and checked, and who verifies it?
10. Who marks the safety intro on a profile (Marta, any volunteer), and does it ever expire?
11. Retiring a tool: who decides it is beyond fixing, and can a tool be retired from any state? Can a lost tool be found and put back?
12. What exactly goes in the board report: how many "most borrowed" tools, over which period, whether "late loans" means ever late or still late, whether repair and replacement charges count as money in, and how and on which day it is delivered?
13. The Google Sheet for shifts: is it entirely outside the system, with the counter app only recording who opened the counter? Does a shift record need an end or a picked-from-list name?
14. Ratings: can members skip the rating, and who sees the stars (members in the catalog, the board, only Marta)?
15. Donations: what happens to a rejected donation, does the fixer weigh in on a questionable tool, and what are the categories (fixed list or free)?
16. Opening hours: exact times for Tuesday and Thursday evenings and Saturday mornings, and any holiday closures. What time of day should the system send reminders and apply late fees?
17. "Dead easy" for the counter app: what hardware do volunteers use (tablet, phone scanner), and what counts as easy enough for you to accept?
