## Questions for the owner

1. What are the prices: basic yearly fee, plus yearly fee, power-tool deposit, late fee per day (any cap), and the replacement charge for a lost tool?
2. What does an upgrade from basic to plus cost mid-year, and does the renewal date move? Do loans already out keep their old due date?
3. If a reservation is not picked up on the pickup day, what happens: does it expire, can they come the next open day, do they lose anything?
4. How late is "too long" before a member is blocked, and how long is "a while" to claim a waitlisted tool? While the offer stands, is the tool held for that person alone?
5. When a returned tool is damaged, what happens to the deposit, and who decides, and by what rule, whether the member pays for the repair, and how much?
6. When a tool is declared lost after a month: does the late fee stop, is the deposit used toward the replacement, and what if the tool turns up later?
7. If the yearly auto-renewal payment fails, or a member wants to cancel or downgrade, what should happen to them and to their open loans?
8. Do reservations count toward the "three or five out at once" limit, or only tools physically on loan?
9. The under-18 rule: can under-18s be members at all, and is the age checked at reservation, at pickup, or both?
10. Who marks the safety intro on a profile (Marta or a volunteer), and does it ever expire?
11. Is the rating only for members to see, or for the board and for deciding which tools to retire? Is it optional?
12. Does the app need to read Sam's Google Sheet, or does it only record who is on from the counter app? Are volunteers also members with a card?
13. How does the board receive the monthly report: pushed by email or looked up on screen? Does "money we took in" count refunded deposits and repair charges, and which are the "deposits kept"?
14. What are the exact opening and closing times of each shed session, and how far ahead can a member reserve?
15. Can a member extend a loan or cancel a reservation?
16. What does a not-good donated tool become: returned, recycled, parts?
17. What does "dead easy" mean as a measurable test for the counter app (training, taps, time)? Must the counter work without internet?
