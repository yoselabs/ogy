## Questions for the owner

1. What do basic and plus cost per year, and what is the deposit (one amount or per tool)? Without these nothing can be charged.
2. What does upgrading mid-year cost, and do loans already running get the longer length?
3. How many days late is "too long" before someone is blocked, and how much is the late fee per day (any cap)?
4. When a tool is lost, how much is "the replacement", and is the deposit taken first or kept? And when exactly is a deposit "kept" (for the report)?
5. When a tool is damaged, who decides whether the member pays and how much, and is that charged to the deposit or to their card? Hand tools have no deposit.
6. Is "a month after it was due" thirty days or a calendar month? Can a lost tool still come back, and what happens to the charge then?
7. What if a member does not turn up on the pickup day? Does the tool go back on the shelf that evening?
8. How long is "a while" to claim from the waitlist, and is the tool held for that person meanwhile?
9. If the automatic yearly renewal fails, what happens to the member and their running loans?
10. How do we know someone is eighteen: date of birth at joining? Can under-eighteens be members for hand tools?
11. Do reservations count toward the three/five limit, or only loans out?
12. Does a damaged return still ask for a rating? Is rating skippable, and who sees it?
13. For the report: does "late loans" mean loans ever late, and which money counts as "took in" (are late fees, repair and replacement charges included)?
