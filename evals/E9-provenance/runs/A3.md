## Questions for the owner

1. What are the amounts: basic and plus yearly fees, the power-tool deposit (one amount or per tool), the late fee per day (any cap), and the replacement charge? None are stated, and the report and Stripe flows cannot be built without them.
2. How many days late before a member is blocked ("too long")? And is the lost clock one calendar month or 30 days after the due date?
3. When a tool comes back damaged, what happens to the deposit: kept, partly kept, or held until Marta decides? Who decides what the member pays for repair, how much, and how is it collected? What if the member refuses?
4. How long is "a while" to claim a waitlisted tool? While the first person holds the offer, can anyone else reserve it? Does a claim need a pickup day?
5. What does an upgrade cost (full plus fee, difference, prorated), and does the renewal date change? What happens when the yearly auto-renewal payment fails, and can a member cancel or downgrade?
6. How do you know someone is eighteen: stated at joining or checked against ID? Who checks, and where?
7. Do reservations count toward the 3/5 limit, or only tools physically out? How far ahead can people reserve, can they cancel, and what if they miss the pickup day?
8. How are late fees and blocks cleared: pay on Stripe in the app, or at the counter? Can a volunteer waive a fee?
9. Does the safety intro ever expire, and may anyone besides Marta mark it?
10. "Late" in the board report: ever late, or late at month end, counted in the month due or the month returned? Is "fees" late fees plus repair charges plus replacements? How is the report delivered, and how many "most borrowed" tools?
11. What is the shift record used for, and does the app ever read Sam's Google Sheet, or is it only kept by Sam?
12. Are ratings optional, and does anything use them (catalog average, hiding low-rated tools)?
13. What do you mean by "dead easy": which counter actions must be possible without training, and is there anything you would rather leave out to stay simple?
14. What happens with a donation that is not good enough, and who prints and sticks the QR sticker? Is there a fixed category list?
15. What are the exact opening hours and holiday closures? Can a lost tool turn up again? Can a tool be returned outside opening hours?
