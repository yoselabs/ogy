# Run B5: tool library capture

Conventions used below. `marks:` carries the mark of a part that differs from its entry; `origins:` carries the mark of each
optional slot that was filled. Local step and end names inside a workflow file are addressed as `<workflow>.<step>`.
Where the spec has no slot for a thing, a YAML comment says so. `origin` is never written.

## Questions for the owner

1. Prices and currency: what is the yearly fee for basic and for plus, what is the power-tool deposit (one amount for every power tool, or per tool), and what is the late fee for each late day? In which currency?
2. When someone is late, is the late fee capped (for example at the tool's replacement price), and after how many days late do you block them from borrowing?
3. When a tool comes back damaged: who decides what the member pays (Marta?), is the deposit used toward the repair or kept or returned, and how does a member pay a repair or late fee at the counter (card through Stripe, cash)?
4. If the yearly renewal card fails, do you try again for a while and then end the membership, end it at once, or keep them until someone follows up? Can tools already out still be returned?
5. When a basic member upgrades to plus part-way through the year, what do they pay, and does their renewal date stay or restart?
6. When a tool is lost after a month, how is its replacement price set (per tool when it is added, or per category), and does the member get money back if the tool turns up later?
7. How do you want to know a member is eighteen or over: date of birth at joining, a volunteer checking ID at the counter, or the member declaring it? Is the age check made when reserving, at the counter, or both?
8. When a tool comes back and someone is waiting, how long do they have to claim it, is the tool held for them meanwhile, and what happens when the time runs out (next person emailed)?
9. What exact times does the shed open and close on Tuesday evening, Thursday evening and Saturday morning?
10. If a reserved tool is not collected on its pickup day, does the reservation end and the tool become available again, or is it held longer?
11. Can a basic member borrow no power tool at all? Does the limit of three or five tools out at once count only tools on loan, or reservations too?
12. A tool comes back both damaged and late: is the late fee still charged? Can a volunteer start a loan for a tool nobody reserved (someone at the counter who wants it now)?
13. The monthly report: how does the board receive it (a page or an email), does "fees" mean late fees only or repair and replacement charges as well, and does "how many loans were late" count loans returned late or loans late at report time?
14. Who marks the safety intro on a member's profile (Marta or any volunteer)? Does it expire or is it done once for life?
15. Is the member emailed when they are blocked? Is a late member emailed anything else besides the day-before reminder?
16. Ratings: can a member skip it, and what are the stars used for (shown in the catalog, shown to the board, only kept)?
17. Donated tool that is not good: what happens to it? Who decides what is "good", and does a donor get any receipt or thanks?
18. The shift sign-up sheet that Sam keeps: should the app ever read it or replace it, or stay completely separate? What does the app do with "who is on" once recorded?
19. Can a member leave or be removed, and what happens to their loans and profile? Is a retired tool or a lost tool ever brought back?
20. What would make the counter app "dead easy" in a way you could check: a new volunteer starting a loan with no training, in how many steps?
