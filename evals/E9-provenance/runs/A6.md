# E9 run A6: tool library capture

Convention used for provenance (the spec has no field for it, so this is my extension): every entry or part carries
`from: [n, ...]`, the owner sentence numbers it came from. An entry with no `from` is an agent proposal (layer
technical/implementation) and says so with `layer`. Everything here is `status: proposed` until the owner confirms the
read-back.

## Questions for the owner

1. Money you have not given numbers for: the two yearly fees, the power-tool deposit (one amount or per tool), the late fee per day (and is there a cap), and the replacement charge (per tool, or fixed)?
2. How late is "too long" before a member is blocked? And is "a month" for lost exactly thirty days after the due date, or a calendar month?
3. What does the member owe when you unblock them: only the late fees, or also anything else? Is the block lifted as soon as the tool is back and the fees are paid, whichever tool it was?
4. A power tool comes back damaged. Do you keep the deposit, or release it and charge a separate repair cost? Your report mentions "deposits kept"; when does that happen? Who decides the amount, Marta or the volunteer?
5. A tool is lost and you charge the replacement. Do you also keep the deposit, and do the late fees stop at that moment?
6. How long is "a while" to claim the tool from the waitlist, and what should happen when the time runs out: the next person is offered it? Is the tool held for the first person meanwhile? Does claiming mean they reserve it for a pickup day?
7. A member reserves a tool and does not come on the pickup day. How long is it held, can they cancel, and how far ahead can they reserve? Can someone reserve a tool that is currently out on loan?
8. Upgrading from basic to plus: what do they pay (full fee, the difference, pro-rated), does the renewal date change, and do loans already out get the longer length? Can anyone go back from plus to basic?
9. The automatic yearly renewal fails, or a member wants to leave. What happens to their membership and to tools they still have?
10. How do you know someone is under eighteen: a birth date at join, checked by whom? May under-eighteens be members at all (basic, hand tools)?
11. Who may mark the safety intro as done: only Marta or any volunteer? Does it ever expire? Do members sign up for the monthly session in the app?
12. For the board report: how many "most borrowed tools", and what exactly is "money we took in" (are repair and replacement charges in it, and is the held deposit left out)? When in the month, and delivered how?
