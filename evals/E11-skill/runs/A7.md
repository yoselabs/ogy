## Owner questions

1. When a delivery fails because nobody is home, how long do you keep the box waiting for the family's answer, and what happens to it if they never answer?
2. When the connection is gone, what must a packer or a driver still be able to do on their own, such as ticking a box or tapping "delivered", and what can wait until it is back?
3. If an item runs short and nothing in its food group is left, what goes in the box? Which food groups do you use?
4. A family has an order in, then their address proof runs out before Wednesday noon. Is the box still packed?
5. If nobody signs up for a route by Friday evening, or no driver is free, what should happen and who should be told? (And what time is "Friday evening"?)
6. If a text to a family does not go through, what should happen? Do you want anyone told, or should it try again?
7. Does "over five hundred" mean one single donation or a donor's total, and in which currency?

## Assumed for read-back

1. Can a family change or cancel an order before cutoff? Assumed: yes before Wednesday noon, no after.
2. Who scans supplier deliveries in and packed boxes out? Assumed: the packers scan boxes out; any warehouse volunteer scans deliveries in.
3. What "roughly when" does the Friday text say? Assumed: morning or afternoon, taken from the route, sent Friday at noon.
4. What if a route has more than twelve stops? Assumed: it is blocked; extra stops go to another route, Priya is told.
5. Do drivers see stops in a fixed order, and can they skip? Assumed: fixed order shown, but any stop can be tapped.
6. How often is Priya reminded while an item stays low? Assumed: once on the drop, again each Monday while still under two weeks.
7. How far ahead of the six-month renewal are families reminded, and by what means? Assumed: a text two weeks before.
8. Who is told if the nightly backup fails, and does the summary still go out? Assumed: Priya is told; the summary still goes out.
9. How is "under two weeks of boxes" measured: from the last week's orders or the average? Assumed: from the average of the last four weeks of orders.
10. What if the renewal proof is rejected? Assumed: the family is told and may upload again; they stay lapsed meanwhile.
11. Is the tax receipt per donation or yearly? Assumed: one receipt per donation, with the donation.
12. Does the director's call get logged as done? Assumed: the director marks it done in the app; if not done in 3 days Priya is told.
13. What does "reliable" mean in numbers? Assumed: no number; reliability means nothing started is silently lost.
14. What do families who signed up but never ordered get? Assumed: nothing.
15. Can one person hold several roles (Priya as coordinator and volunteer)? Assumed: yes.
16. Are late orders after Wednesday noon refused or queued for next week? Assumed: refused, with a message to come back Monday.
17. Is a family's address ever wrong at the door? Assumed: the driver taps "nobody home".
18. Are the board summary and backup kept how long? Assumed: backups for 30 days; the owner confirms.
