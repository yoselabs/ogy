## Owner questions

1. When nobody is home, the box comes back and the family gets a text. How long do you keep the box if they never answer, and what happens to it then (perishable food)?
2. When a family or a donor gets a text or email and it fails to send (Twilio or Stripe is down), what should we do and who should be told? Includes: a payment that goes through but the thank-you or tax receipt does not.
3. The warehouse wifi is patchy. When it drops, must packers ticking boxes and drivers tapping "delivered" keep working and send later, or is "try again when connected" acceptable?
4. "Over five hundred" for big donors: is that one gift, or a person's total? And how should the director find out so the call happens (email, a list on a screen)?
5. If an item is short and nothing in the same food group can replace it, what should the packing list do? And which food groups do you use?
6. If no driver signs up for a route by Friday evening, who is told and what then? What time is "Friday evening", and when is the Friday text sent?
7. Can a family change or cancel its order before Wednesday noon, and what if its eligibility lapses after it ordered?

## Assumed for read-back

- Friday evening = 18:00 for route sign-up; Friday text goes at 12:00 to every family with an order that week. Confirm or correct.
- Order window: opens Monday 00:00 and closes Wednesday 12:00, in the food bank's local time. Confirm.
- Order change/cancel before cutoff is allowed; after cutoff, orders are refused (no late orders). Confirm.
- An order placed while eligible is still packed and delivered if eligibility lapses afterward. Confirm.
- Family size is stored on the family account, not entered per order. Confirm.
- Large box applies when family size is 5 or more ("more than four"). Derived from [4].
- Priya is a person in the coordinator role; the coordinator both assigns routes and receives low-stock emails. Derived from [8], [16].
- Low-stock email goes once each time an item drops under two weeks of boxes (not repeated). Confirm.
- "Two weeks of boxes" is measured from the current weekly order totals. Confirm.
- Substitution is only within the same food group, never across groups; if none, the item is left out and Priya is told. Confirm.
- Food groups: grains, protein, produce, dairy, tinned. Confirm.
- Nobody-home box is held at the warehouse until Monday, then given back to stock/donated. Confirm (see question 1).
- Pick-up text is sent once; no repeated retries. Confirm.
- A failed Twilio text is retried every 5 minutes for 1 hour, then the coordinator is told. Confirm.
- A failed Stripe payment shows an error to the donor; no thank-you is sent; nothing is recorded as a donation. Confirm.
- A thank-you with tax receipt is sent once per donation; if sending fails it is retried, never duplicated. Confirm.
- Donations are one-time money gifts; no recurring gifts, no refunds handled by the system. Confirm.
- Who checks proof of address: any volunteer given that permission; family sees accept or reject with a reason. Confirm.
- Rejected proof: family is told and may upload again; eligibility stays as before. Confirm.
