## Owner questions

1. When something on the packing list is short and nothing else from the same food group can replace it, what should happen: leave the item out and tell Priya, or hold the whole box until she decides?
2. If a driver taps "nobody home" and the family never answers the text about picking up, what happens to the box, and how long do we keep it?
3. The warehouse wifi is patchy: if it drops while packers are ticking off boxes, should they be able to keep going and have it catch up later, and how long can that go on?
4. If nobody has signed up for a route by Friday evening, what should happen, and what time Friday evening is the cutoff?
5. When a family's six months run out after they have already ordered, does that order still get delivered, and should we warn families before they lapse?
6. "Big donors, over five hundred": is that one gift over five hundred, or a donor's total across gifts, and in which currency?
7. If the nightly backup fails, who should be told, and should the board summary still go out?

## Assumed for read-back

1. Monday ordering opens at 00:00. Confirm?
2. Friday sign-up closes at 18:00. Confirm?
3. The ordinary box is called "standard", and only two sizes exist. Confirm?
4. Warehouse volunteers scan supplier deliveries in (the owner named no role). Confirm?
5. One route per driver, and the coordinator is emailed Friday evening about any uncovered route (if Q4 is not answered more fully).
6. The Friday text tells families a rough window computed from the route's start time and their stop position, and families without a phone number get no text.
7. "Under two weeks of boxes" is measured per item as on-hand stock divided by the recent weekly box use; Priya gets one email per item when it first drops under and not again until it recovers.
8. A family that is lapsed sees "renew first" when trying to order; renewal takes effect when a volunteer approves the proof, and lasts six months from approval.
9. A rejected proof tells the family why in a message, and they can upload again.
10. Orders placed Monday to Wednesday noon can be changed or cancelled by the family until the cutoff; after the cutoff they cannot.
11. Packed boxes take their items out of stock at the tick, not at the packing list.
12. Twilio text failures are retried up to 3 times, then the coordinator is told; texts are never sent twice for the same event (rerun: checks).
13. A Stripe result of "unknown" is checked again before any thank-you is sent; the donor is never charged twice.
14. The thank-you email and tax receipt go out immediately after payment, once per donation.
15. The director gets the donor's name and phone number for the call; no deadline for the call.
16. The nightly summary covers the previous day only, and goes to the board by email.
17. The backup is kept for 30 days (no source; board can change).
18. Driver app works on any phone's browser, no install needed beyond a link; large buttons and text.
