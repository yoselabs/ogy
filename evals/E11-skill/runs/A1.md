## Assumed for read-back

Each item is in force and shows as assumed until the owner confirms.

- Orders open Monday at 00:00. (decision/times-and-currency)
- "Friday evening" signup deadline is 18:00. (same)
- The Friday "roughly when" text goes at 12:00, to every family with a box on a route that week, with a time window taken from the route order. (same; decision/eta-source)
- Currency is the local currency. (same)
- A family can change or cancel its order until the cutoff; after the cutoff, no changes. (not in the words)
- A family without an order in the week does not get the Friday text and is not on the packing list.
- A new family is eligible only after the first proof is checked. (decision/first-sign-up)
- A rejected proof leaves the family lapsed (or eligible until its existing date if still valid), and the family is told by text. Who tells them is the system via Twilio.
- Nobody is warned before eligibility lapses; the family is only told on trying to order. (a reminder would be an addition; not captured)
- Priya is a person in the coordinator role; another person with that role can do everything she does.
- Packers (or warehouse volunteers) scan deliveries in; packing a box removes its items from stock. (decision/who-does-stock-work)
- The "swap" happens when the packing list is built after the cutoff; the family is not told about swaps. The coordinator sees a list of swaps.
- If no item in the same group is in stock, the item is left out and the coordinator is told. (decision/no-substitute; also Question 5 gives the final answer)
- Food groups: grains, protein, produce, dairy, canned goods. (decision/food-groups)
- Every box item has a fixed amount per box (the box recipe); "two weeks of boxes" is computed from the average orders of the last four weeks. (decision/weeks-measure, decision/box-recipe)
- The low-stock email goes once per item per low spell; it resets once stock recovers.
- A box that comes back is given back to stock only if not perishable; otherwise given away. (part of decision/nobody-home-fate)
- Text to the family for "nobody home" goes once; if Twilio does not answer, retry up to 3 times over the next hour, then the coordinator is told. (failure behaviour not in the words; rerun: checks)
- A failed or unknown Stripe payment: no thank-you, no receipt, the donor sees an error and can retry.
- Thank-you and receipt are sent once per paid donation; repeated charges are not sent twice (rerun: never).
- The tax receipt is a PDF or formatted email with amount, date, organization name; format and tax rules are not captured here.
- Reliability target: no order, scan, tick or delivery mark is lost; every one survives a connection loss and a restart.
- Driver app: one tap per result, big buttons, no typing, no login more than once. (quality/simple_driver_app)
- The route stop limit is checked when the coordinator assigns families to a route; extra families need another route.
- Stop order on a route is chosen by the coordinator (or system by area) and fixed; the driver cannot reorder.
- Drivers can mark stops delivered only on Saturday (the delivery day).
- The nightly backup holds all data; the summary covers the period since the last summary.
- The board summary goes to every board member by email, with counts only (orders, deliveries, donations), no names.
- A volunteer who checks proofs sees the uploaded file and the family's name and address, nothing else.
- Families with more than four people always get the large box; there is no other box size and no family chooses a box.
