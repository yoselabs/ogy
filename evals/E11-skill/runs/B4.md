## Owner questions

1. When a text to a family fails or we cannot tell if it was sent, who should step in? And what time on Friday should the delivery text go out, and roughly what should it say?
2. After a driver taps "nobody home", how long does the family have to answer the pickup text, and what happens to the box if they say no or never answer?
3. If a card payment fails or we are not sure it went through, what should the donor see? When a donor gives over five hundred, do they still get the thank-you email as well as the director's call, and is that five hundred in one gift or over a year?
4. When the driver's phone or the warehouse tablet loses signal, what absolutely must still work (for example, tapping "delivered" and sending it later)? What would "reliable" mean to you in one sentence?
5. If a family's six months run out after they ordered this week, do they still get this week's box?
6. If we are short on an item and nothing else in its food group is left, what goes in the box, and who is told?
7. If the nightly backup or the board summary fails, who should be told?

## Assumed for read-back

- Friday evening deadline for route sign-up means 18:00. (decision/times)
- Friday text goes at 10:00 with a four-hour window; it is sent to every family with an order. (also asked in Q1)
- A failed or unclear text: retry for an hour, then the coordinator phones the family. (Q1)
- Pickup answer deadline is Monday 12:00; no answer or "no" means the box is closed unclaimed (food is returned to stock) rather than offered again. (Q2)
- Failed payment: the donor sees "not completed, you were not charged"; no automatic retry; an uncertain Stripe answer is treated as not paid until Stripe confirms. (Q3)
- Big-donor limit is one gift, in the local currency, strictly above 500; thank-you email is still sent; the director gets an email with the donor's contact. (Q3)
- Offline: taps and ticks are saved on the device and sent when signal returns; nothing is lost or done twice. (Q4)
- A lapse after ordering does not cancel this week's order. (Q5)
- A short item with no same-group substitute: the box is packed without it and the coordinator is told. (Q6)
- Priya is told once per item until it is restocked, not daily. (Q6)
- "Weeks of boxes" is reckoned from the average of the last four weeks' orders.
- A proof waits at most three days for a volunteer, then the coordinator is told.
- A volunteer who rejects a proof gives a short reason shown to the family; the family may upload again at any time.
- Failed backup or board email: the coordinator is emailed next morning. (Q7)
- Who covers when Priya is away: any person with the coordinator role; a route with no driver on Saturday morning is reported to the coordinator Friday night. 
- Families who lapse can still upload a proof and view their status; they only cannot place an order.
- Order changes or cancellation by the family before cutoff are allowed (the owner said one box a week, nothing about changing); after cutoff they are not.
- The large box depends on family size recorded at order time; a family changing its size updates it at the next order only.
- The family receives no text on delivered; only on "nobody home" and on Friday.
- Number of stops in a route is a cap, a route may have fewer; drivers sign up for one route per Saturday.
- Packers tick boxes one at a time; ticking a box takes its items out of stock then, and a wrong tick can be undone by the packer until the boxes leave.
- Stock is counted per item; scanning in a supplier delivery adds to it; the stock count is not shared outside the warehouse app.
- Warehouse tablet is a single shared iPad; no per-packer login. Drivers and volunteers have accounts.
- Backup is a copy kept off the machine; summary covers the last 24 hours.
- The tax receipt is for the full donation amount; no recurring donations; no refunds in the app.
- Hazard restart: unfinished work continues after a restart, with sends checked for "already sent" before being repeated.
- Cutoff is Wednesday noon in the food bank's local time; nobody can order outside the window, not even a coordinator.
