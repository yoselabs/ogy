## Owner questions

1. When the wifi or phone signal drops, in the warehouse or on a route, what still has to work? For example, can packers tick boxes and drivers tap "delivered" with no signal and have it sync later, or is a printed list the backup? (decision/offline)
2. When nobody is home and the family gets the text asking about pickup: how long do you hold the box, and what if they never answer or the text fails to send? (decision/nobody-home)
3. When someone donates and the thank-you email fails, or the same donation arrives twice, what should happen? And does the director call happen on a donation of exactly 500, and in what currency? (decision/donations)
4. If a family's six months run out partway through the week, can the order they already placed still be packed? Is a new family allowed to order before a volunteer checks their address, and who tells a family their proof was refused? (decision/eligibility)
5. When a food group runs out completely so the packing list has no substitute, what should the packers see, and who is told? (decision/stock-rules)
6. If a delivery route has no driver by Friday evening, what do you do, and what time do you mean by "Friday evening"? (decision/routes)
7. If the nightly backup fails, should the board still get the summary, and who gets told? (decision/reporting)

## Assumed for read-back

- Friday text goes out Friday at 16:00 and gives a time window per route. (decision/deadlines, assumed)
- If a Friday text fails, never send twice and list failures for the coordinator. (decision/texts)
- A family can change or cancel an order until the cutoff. (decision/ordering-rules)
- Orders are a week's box, "one a week" is enforced per family per calendar week.
- "Under two weeks of boxes" is strictly less; "over five hundred" is strictly more; "more than four people" is size > 4.
- Low-stock email goes once on crossing the threshold, again if still low a week later. (decision/stock-rules)
- The nightly summary covers the last 24 hours and goes to the whole board list. (decision/reporting)
- Scanning supplier deliveries is done by warehouse staff on the tablet.
- A returned box waits until the next packing day; contents are not perishable-safe beyond that.
- Priya is a holder of the coordinator role, not a separate persona; her email address is configuration.
- A driver can take a route by themselves, or the coordinator assigns one; either way the route has one driver.
- Packing list is regenerated from orders only once, at cutoff; orders after cutoff are not allowed.
- Packers are also the people who scan stock in; no separate warehouse persona.
- Payment currency is the local currency of the food bank.
- Stripe/Twilio/email versions are pinned by the agent (technical layer, owner ratifies).
- Backup failures are reported to the coordinator, not only to the board.
