3. When a text or an email cannot be sent, or we cannot tell whether it arrived (Friday text, pickup text, thank-you with tax receipt, Priya's low-stock email, the board summary), who do we tell and how hard do we try? (I would retry for a few hours, then tell the coordinator.)
4. When a family's six months run out while it has a box order waiting, do we cancel that order or let this week's go ahead and block the next one? And do we warn families two weeks before? (I would keep this week's, block the next, and warn.)

Does the coordinator build the routes (group the stops) and drivers pick one, or do drivers only offer and Priya chooses who gets which route? ## Assumed for read-back

Times and numbers
- Monday opening time: 00:00, the start of Monday.
- "Friday evening" for route sign-up: 18:00.
- Friday "when to expect us" text goes at 10:00 and gives a morning or afternoon window by the family's place on its route.
- "Two" at night is the food bank's local time zone.
- An order placed at exactly Wednesday 12:00 still counts.
- Currency of the 500: the food bank's local currency.
- "Two weeks of boxes" for an item: on-hand divided by its use in the last 4 weeks of packed boxes; counted per item, not per food group.
- A scan adds one unit; quantity per scan is set when stock is loaded.

People and permissions
- Anyone in the warehouse can scan in a delivery; the packers do.
- A new family is pending and cannot order until a volunteer approves its first proof of address.
- Anyone with the reviewer role (volunteer) can approve or reject proof; there is no deadline for them. After a rejection the family is told what was wrong and may upload again at once.
- The coordinator sets the order of stops on a route; the driver sees them in that order.
- A route that is not finished by evening has no automatic end; the coordinator sees open stops.
- The director is told of a big donation by email with the donor's name, phone and amount; the call is made outside the system and not tracked.

Money and receipts
- One tax receipt layout for every donation; the legal wording is supplied by the owner at build.
- If Stripe's answer is unknown, we do not thank the donor and do not charge again until we have checked with Stripe; a person looks at it later.
- Refunds are done by hand in Stripe.

Messages and reports
- The nightly summary has counts of orders and deliveries, and the number and total of donations, since the last summary.
- If the backup fails, the summary is still sent with a line saying so, and the coordinator is told.
- Email provider is chosen at build.

Reliability
- Packers' ticks, scans and drivers' taps work with no connection and catch up when it returns (derived from [22], [23]); older drivers need two taps and no typing per stop.
- After a restart, missed scheduled jobs (cutoff, Friday text, nightly summary) run once when the system is back, and anything sent is checked before being sent again.
- Unknown email address or phone for a family: the coordinator is told instead of silently skipping.

Hazard grid cells left open (each with proposed answer)
- Place order twice: second tap is refused by "one box a week" (guard).
- Two people place the same family's order at once: only one succeeds.
- Packer ticks the same box twice: the second tick does nothing (idempotent_overwrite); stock is taken once.
- Delivery scanned in twice: asks the person to confirm before adding again; not silently counted twice.
- Driver taps "delivered" then "nobody home" by mistake: the last tap before the route ends wins, with an undo for one minute.
- Pickup text sent twice: allowed, harmless, but a second reply from the family is ignored.
- The low-stock email arrives while it is already low: sent once per drop below two weeks, not daily.
- Backup stops halfway: counted as failed, never as made.
- Stock count drifts from the shelf: the coordinator can set a counter by hand (reset); who does so is not in the owner's words.
