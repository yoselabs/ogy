## Assumed for read-back

- A family can change or cancel an order until the cutoff; after the cutoff the packing list is fixed.
- Orders placed with no eligibility check beyond the lapsed flag; a lapse happens exactly six months after the last accepted proof.
- A family's eligibility lapses on a schedule the system keeps; the family is not warned in advance. Proposed: a text two weeks before (not said, so assumed and shown as assumed).
- Who builds the routes (groups families into them): the coordinator (Priya).
- Who scans supplier deliveries in: a packer using the warehouse app.
- Driver app phone with no signal: taps are kept on the phone and sent when signal is back; drivers see stops without needing a connection.
- Text delivery fails (Twilio down): retry a few times, then the coordinator sees which families were not reached. Details are technical.
- Pickup text gets no answer: the box returns to the pool and the family order is closed as undelivered for the week (see decision/nobody-home).
- Low-stock email goes once per item until stock recovers; "two weeks of boxes" is measured against the current week's packing list.
- Big-donor call: the director gets an email naming the donor and the amount; no reminder.

- Renewal: the family is told the result by text and may upload again if rejected.
- Nightly summary covers the previous 24 hours; the backup keeps a limited history (technical, owner is not asked).
- The system is down at 02:00 (hazard restart): the backup and summary run on next start, once.
- A family can only have one order a week even if the order failed to send twice (a duplicate click does not make two orders).
- Roles: Priya is a coordinator; others may hold that role later. A role holds, not a person.
