## Owner questions

1. When nobody is home and the family does not answer the pick-up text, how long do you keep the box before you give up on it, and what happens to the food then?
2. When the warehouse wifi drops, what must still work on the tablet (ticking off boxes, seeing the list), and is it fine for it to catch up later when the signal returns?
3. If we are short on something and there is nothing else in the same food group, what should the packing list do?
4. If a family's eligibility lapses after they have already ordered for that week, does that order still get a box?
5. What time on Friday evening do route sign-ups close, and what should happen if a route has no driver by then?
6. Does "over five hundred" mean one single donation, or what one person gave in total, and in which currency?
7. Can a family change or cancel their order before Wednesday noon, and what if they miss ordering altogether (do they get anything)?

## Assumed for read-back

- Priya is the usual coordinator, role is "coordinator"; others can be coordinator too. Confirm.
- Who scans in supplier deliveries: any volunteer in the warehouse. The warehouse app is a screen, same system as the web app.
- The donor gets the thank-you email and tax receipt; donor is not a persona with an account, just an email address on the donation.
- First sign-up also needs proof of address checked by a volunteer, same as renewal.
- Eligibility lapse is counted six months from the last approval, not calendar-based.
- Orders still unticked at Saturday packing time: coordinator is told Friday noon.
- Friday expected-time text goes at 12:00 with a morning or afternoon window by route order.
- Pickup-instead: link in the text, family answers there; box held until Monday 09:00 then given up.
- Route sign-up: coordinator chooses between drivers; deadline 18:00 Friday; unclaimed route alerts the coordinator.
- If a driver cannot finish a route, the coordinator reassigns remaining stops.
- Low-stock email: once per item until stock rises above the two-week line again; "weeks of boxes" = on-hand divided by last weeks' use per item.
- Large box and standard box are the only sizes; a family can not pick items.
- Nightly summary covers everything since the previous summary; backup failure also tells the board.
- A family without a phone number cannot get texts; phone is required at sign-up.
- Twilio, Stripe and email delivery are never re-sent on a restart (rerun: never) when unsure whether the first went out; the system checks first where it can.
- Large-donor threshold uses local currency; the director is told once per donation.
- Packers use only the iPad; drivers use their own phones; no other device is supported.
- Offline: tick-offs and delivered/nobody-home taps are saved on the device and sent when the connection returns; the packing list is cached once received.
