## Owner questions

1. When nobody is home and the family does not answer the pickup text (or the text can't be sent), what should happen to the box, and how long do we keep it?
2. The warehouse wifi is patchy: if the tablet or a driver's phone has no signal, should packers and drivers keep working and have it catch up later, or is it fine to wait?
3. If an email, text or payment confirmation fails (thank-you or receipt, Friday text, Priya's low-stock email, board summary), who finds out and what do we do?
4. Is "over five hundred" one single donation or a total over time, and are donors told about anything beyond the receipt and the director's call?
5. What happens if a family's eligibility lapses after they have already ordered, and should they be warned before it runs out? What if no volunteer checks the proof?
6. If no driver signs up for a route by Friday evening, or a route has more than twelve stops, what does Priya do?
7. If we are short on something and nothing in the same food group is left, what goes in the box? And what are the food groups?

## Assumed for read-back

- Friday evening means 18:00 (route sign-up close). Confirm?
- The Friday "when to expect us" text goes at 12:00, once per family. Confirm?
- Orders placed outside Monday to Wednesday noon are refused with "ordering is closed"; a second box in the same week is refused. (Concluded from [3], [4].)
- Order time zone is the food bank's local time zone.
- A "family" gets one text number; a household size is entered at signup.
- "Large box" applies when household size is 5 or more ([4]: "more than four").
- The packing list is built once at the cutoff; orders after that wait for next week.
- The packers' tick-offs take items out of stock at that moment ([14]).
- Stock "two weeks of boxes" is measured against the last four weeks of average weekly boxes. Confirm or give another way?
- Priya is the coordinator persona; anyone she designates may assign routes.
- Anyone on the volunteer list may check proof of address; the first to open it checks it.
- Rejected proof of address returns to the family with a reason and they may upload again.
- Eligibility lapse after ordering: the order is still delivered for that week, nothing later (assumed; owner's Q5).
- Eligibility lapse warning: text 14 days before six months end.
- Unassigned route on Friday evening: Priya is emailed at 18:00 and decides.
- A donation under Stripe failure is not recorded and the donor sees an error; no receipt is sent on unknown payment state until Stripe confirms.
- Thank-you email retries until sent (rerun checks), never twice for the same donation.
- Big donors: the director gets one call task per donation over the limit, shown on his screen and by email.
- Nightly backup stored in a place off the main machine; on failure Priya and the board are emailed. Summary covers the previous 24 hours.
