## Owner questions

1. If a text to a family does not go through (Twilio down, wrong number) -- the Friday "when to expect us" text or the "nobody home, want to pick up?" text -- should we try again and tell Priya, tell Priya right away, or just carry on?
2. The warehouse wifi is patchy. If the iPad loses connection while packers are ticking boxes, should they carry on and have it catch up later, or stop until it is back? And how many hours offline is acceptable?
3. If nobody is home and the family never answers the pickup text, what happens to the box: back to stock, or kept for next week?
4. When an item is short and nothing in the same food group is available, what should the packing list do (leave it off, put in something else, ask Priya)?
5. If a route has no driver by Friday evening, who is told, and what happens to the families on it? What clock time is "Friday evening"?
6. For donations: if the payment shows as unclear or the thank-you email fails, should the donor still get a receipt later, and who checks? Is exactly 500 a "big donor" for the director's call, and who makes sure the call actually happens?
7. If the nightly backup fails, who should be told and when? (Or: does an eligibility rejection tell the family why, and are they warned before six months run out?)

## Assumed for read-back

Times and numbers (decision/times):
- "Friday evening" = 18:00. Friday text goes at 12:00 (noon). Driver app: one tap per stop.
- Exactly 500 is not a big donor (strictly over 500, as said).
- Orders open Monday at 00:00 (Monday is the open day; midnight assumed).
- Large box is for 5+ people ("more than four").
- Two weeks of boxes is measured against the last week's orders.

Ordering and packing:
- A family that has already ordered this week cannot order again (second order is refused and they are told why).
- A family that is lapsed at order time is told "renew your eligibility" and cannot order; an order already placed stays valid if they lapse before the cutoff.
- Orders placed after Wednesday noon are refused with a message, not queued for next week.
- An order is never placed twice for the same (family, week) if the app is retried (idempotent).
- The packing list is sent once; if the tablet is offline it is re-sent when the tablet connects. Ticks are saved on the tablet and uploaded later; a tick received twice counts once.
- A packed box that is dropped takes its items out of stock exactly once.
- Stock may not go below zero; a scan-out below zero is flagged to Priya.

Delivery:
- A route with no driver by Friday evening is flagged to the coordinator that evening; the coordinator can drive or reassign it.
- A driver app that loses signal saves taps and syncs later; "delivered" tapped twice counts once.
- A stop marked "nobody home" can be corrected by the driver the same day.
- If the family says they want to pick up, the box waits at the warehouse until the next Saturday, then returns to stock.
- A route has at most 12 stops; the 13th is put on another route.
- Pickup texts go out when "nobody home" is tapped, not batched.

Stock:
- Low-stock email goes once per item until stock is back above two weeks, not daily.
- Supplier deliveries scanned twice add stock once (duplicate scan refused).
- Item swaps keep the same box size and are shown on the packing list.

Donations:
- Payment unclear = not paid; no receipt; retried by Stripe's own events.
- Thank-you/receipt email failure is retried up to 3 times, then Priya sees it in a list.
- Receipts are sent once per payment even if Stripe sends the event twice.
- The director is told of a big donation the same day; call done is ticked by the director.

Eligibility:
- Family is warned 2 weeks before the six months run out.
- Rejected proof: family is told the reason and can upload again.
- Six months counted from approval.

Nightly:
- Backup failure emails the coordinator in the morning; no summary is sent on a failed night (or it is sent saying "backup failed").
- Summary covers the last 24 hours.
- Only the board receives it.

Hazard grid cells left open (assumed na or default hazards as above): double order submit (idempotent_overwrite), double scan (assumed one-time), restart mid-delivery (resume from saved stop results), Stripe event delivered twice (checks), two coordinators assigning the same route at once (conflict: second one refused).
