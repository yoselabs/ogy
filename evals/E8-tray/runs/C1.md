- Is the room held while waiting for approval?
- How long is the room held waiting for payment, and what happens when it expires?
- If payment fails or Stripe is down, what does the member see; can they retry?
- Can a member cancel while payment is pending, and is the hold released?
- How long before the start is a cancellation "late"?
- How much is the late fee, and how is it charged (Stripe card on file)?
- On cancel, are payments refunded or credits restored; does it differ by early/late?
- Do premium members also pay the late fee, or lose credits instead?
- How many credits does a booking cost; what if credits are insufficient?
- When premium credits are deducted at confirmation, what if the room becomes unavailable meanwhile?
- Do premium members need host approval for big rooms?
- Is the door code created by us or Kisi; is it per booking, and when does it expire?
- What if SMS or Kisi fails after confirmation; does the booking still count as confirmed?
- Does the member see the door code in the app too?
- When exactly does a booking become "finished" (slot end time, or manual)?
- How is the cleaner told, and what if the room is not cleaned; can the room be rebooked meanwhile?
- Who sets up rooms, capacities and members' plans (admin tooling)?
- What does usage history show (finished only, cancelled too, fees, credits) and over what period?
- Who can read a note, can the host reply, and can notes be edited or added after the meeting?
- Can members change a booking (time, room, attendees) instead of cancel and rebook?
- Is there a maximum booking length or lead time; can bookings be made in the past?
- Are refunds, disputes or no-shows handled; and what if Stripe or Kisi changes things behind us?
