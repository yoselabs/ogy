## Questions for the owner

- What counts as a "late" cancellation (hours before start)?
- How much is the late fee, in what currency, and do premium members pay it in credits?
- What makes a room "bigger"; is it capacity, or a flag per room? Does attendee count or room size trigger approval?
- How many credits does a booking cost (per hour, per room)? Do premium members ever pay with Stripe (not enough credits)?
- Are credits returned on cancel? On late cancel are credits lost?
- Is a paid booking refunded on timely cancellation? Who triggers it?
- How long is a room held while waiting for payment?
- How long may the host take to approve, and what happens on no answer? Is payment asked before or after approval (stated: host approves first)?
- What if the host declines: does the member hear, and how?
- How is the price computed (room, duration)?
- Is the Kisi code revoked on cancel? What if Kisi or SMS is down or rejects: confirm anyway?
- How does the booking become "finished": automatically at end time, or marked by someone?
- When is the credit deducted for approval-needed rooms (stated: at confirmation)?
- How is the cleaner notified and is there a deadline? Does anything wait on cleaning?
- Who manages rooms, capacities, member plans, phone numbers and credit top-ups (out of scope?)
- Can a member change a booking (time, attendees), or only cancel?
- Note: length limit, editable, allowed for cancelled/finished bookings, does the host get notified?
- Is usage history only bookings, or also fees and credits spent? Any filtering/export?
- What happens on Stripe refunds, disputes or declines; payment hold when Stripe is down?
- Max booking length, opening hours, how far ahead can members book?
- Can the host also cancel or edit bookings? Who is the person that handles a failed late fee?
