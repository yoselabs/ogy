# Capture A3: meeting rooms

## Entries

```yaml
persona:
  member:         {nature: human, ?open: {question: "Is a member who books always the same as the person attending?", assumed: "yes"}}
  community-host: {nature: human}
  cleaner:        {nature: human}
external:
  stripe:       {owns_facts: [payment status], events: [payment-confirmed]}
  sms-provider: {owns_facts: [text delivery]}
  kisi:         {owns_facts: [door codes on the lock]}
term:
  slot:      {means: "a room and a time period a member picks"}
  door-code: {means: "code that opens the room door for a confirmed booking"}
  late-cancellation: {means: "a cancellation made less than value/late-cancel-window before the booking starts",
    ?open: {question: "How is late defined?", assumed: "less than the window before start"}}
  big-room: {means: "a room whose capacity is above value/big-room-capacity"}
goal:
  book-meeting-room: {states: "a member has a room for a time and can enter it"}
  see-usage:         {states: "a member can see what they used"}
set:
  plan: {members: [standard, premium]}
  ?open: {question: "Are there plans other than standard and premium?", assumed: "no"}
value:
  hold-duration:     {is: duration, unit: minutes, ?open: {question: "How long is a room held while waiting for payment?", assumed: "none stated, undecided"}}
  late-cancel-window: {is: duration, unit: hours, ?open: {question: "How close to the start is a cancellation late?", assumed: "undecided"}}
  late-fee:          {is: number, unit: money, ?open: {question: "How much is the late fee (fixed, or a share of the price)?", assumed: "undecided"}}
  big-room-capacity: {is: number, unit: people, ?open: {question: "From what capacity does a room need host approval?", assumed: "undecided"}}
  booking-credits:   {is: number, unit: credits, ?open: {question: "How many credits does a booking cost (per hour, per room)?", assumed: "undecided"}}
entity:
  room:
    attrs: {name: {type: text}, capacity: {type: number}}
    key: [name]
    ?open: {question: "Price per room or per time?", assumed: "price is an attribute of the room, undecided"}
  member:
    attrs: {name: {type: text}, plan: {type: set/plan}, credits: {type: counter}}
    ?open: {question: "How do premium members get credits and how many?", assumed: "out of scope, credits is only read and decreased"}
  booking:
    attrs:
      member: {type: entity/member}
      room: {type: entity/room}
      starts: {type: time}
      ends: {type: time}
      attendees: {type: number}
      note: {type: text, optional: true}
      door-code: {type: text, optional: true}
      late-fee-charged: {type: bool}
    lifecycle:
      states: [pending-payment, confirmed, cancelled, finished]
      initial: pending-payment
      terminal: [cancelled, finished]
      transitions:
        pay:     {from: pending-payment, to: confirmed}
        cancel:  {from: [pending-payment, confirmed], to: cancelled}
        finish:  {from: confirmed, to: finished}
      ?open: {question: "Where does a big-room booking waiting for host approval sit, as the owner names only four states, and what if the host declines?",
        assumed: "approval happens before pending-payment, outside the four states; decline ends in cancelled"}
derived:
  needs-approval: {is: "room.capacity > value/big-room-capacity"}
  room-free: {is: "no other booking for the room in [starts..ends] has state pending-payment or confirmed",
    ?open: {question: "Does a held (pending) booking block the room?", assumed: "yes, the owner says we hold it"}}
workflow:
  book-room:
    goal: goal/book-meeting-room
    actors: [persona/member, persona/community-host, external/stripe, system]
    trigger: {actor: persona/member}
    inputs: {room: entity/room, starts: time, ends: time, attendees: number}
    start: check-room
    ends:
      booked:   {result: success, means: "booking is confirmed and the door code is sent"}
      refused:  {result: failure, means: "room taken or booking not approved or not paid"}
    steps:
      check-room:
        actor: system
        cases:
          taken: {order: 1, when: "not derived/room-free", then: end/refused}
          big:   {order: 2, when: "derived/needs-approval", then: step/host-approval}
        otherwise: {then: step/hold, ?open: {question: "Is attendees above room capacity refused?", assumed: "yes, refused as room taken"}}
      host-approval:
        actor: persona/community-host
        choices:
          approve: {label: Approve, then: step/hold}
          decline: {label: Decline, then: end/refused}
        ?timeout: {after: value/host-approval-wait, then: end/refused, ?open: {question: "What if the host does not answer, and how long may the member wait?", assumed: "no timeout"}}
      hold:
        actor: system
        then: step/payment
        causes: [{create: booking}]
      payment:
        actor: external/stripe
        choices:
          paid:   {label: "Stripe confirms", then: step/plan-check}
          failed: {label: "Payment failed", then: end/refused}
        timeout: {after: value/hold-duration, then: end/refused}
        ?open: {question: "Premium members use credits instead of paying: do they skip Stripe, and is the booking then confirmed at once?", assumed: "premium skips payment, confirmation happens at once"}
      plan-check:
        actor: system
        cases:
          premium: {order: 1, when: "member.plan = premium", then: step/deduct}
        otherwise: {then: step/confirm}
      deduct:
        actor: system
        then: step/confirm
        causes: [{count: member.credits, op: dec}]
        conflict: {then: end/refused}
        ?open: {question: "What if a premium member has too few credits?", assumed: "refused"}
      confirm:
        actor: system
        then: step/send-code
        causes: [{transition: booking.pay}]
      send-code:
        actor: system
        then: end/booked
        causes: [{write: booking.door-code}, {tell: external/sms-provider, what: door code}, {tell: external/kisi, what: door code}]
        ?open: {question: "If the SMS or Kisi call fails, what happens?", assumed: "nothing specified; booking stays confirmed"}
  cancel-booking:
    goal: goal/book-meeting-room
    actors: [persona/member, system]
    trigger: {actor: persona/member}
    inputs: {booking: entity/booking}
    start: check-late
    ends:
      cancelled: {result: success, means: "booking is cancelled"}
    steps:
      check-late:
        actor: system
        cases:
          late: {order: 1, when: "booking.starts - now < value/late-cancel-window and state/booking = confirmed", then: step/charge-fee}
        otherwise: {then: step/cancel}
      charge-fee:
        actor: external/stripe
        then: step/cancel
        causes: [{write: booking.late-fee-charged}]
        ?open: {question: "Late fee by Stripe or by credits for premium?", assumed: "premium pays the fee in credits"}
      cancel:
        actor: system
        then: end/cancelled
        causes: [{transition: booking.cancel}]
        ?open: {question: "Are credits returned when a premium member cancels, and the payment refunded on a timely cancel?", assumed: "undecided"}
  finish-booking:
    goal: goal/book-meeting-room
    actors: [system, persona/cleaner]
    trigger: {after: value/booking-length, since: transition/booking.pay}
    ?open: {question: "What ends a booking: its end time? Who or what marks it finished?", assumed: "the end time, by the system"}
    start: finish
    ends: {done: {result: success, means: "room is ready"}}
    steps:
      finish: {actor: system, then: step/clean, causes: [{transition: booking.finish}]}
      clean:
        actor: persona/cleaner
        choices: {ready: {label: "Room is ready", then: end/done}}
        ?open: {question: "How is the cleaner told, and must the room be ready before the next booking?", assumed: "the cleaner is told when the booking finishes, no deadline"}
  add-note:
    goal: goal/book-meeting-room
    actors: [persona/member, persona/community-host]
    trigger: {actor: persona/member}
    inputs: {booking: entity/booking, note: text}
    start: write
    ends: {saved: {result: success, means: "note is on the booking"}}
    steps:
      write:
        actor: persona/member
        choices: {save: {label: "Save note", then: end/saved}}
        causes: [{write: booking.note}, {tell: persona/community-host, what: note}]
        ?open: {question: "Until when can a note be added (cancelled or finished bookings)? Is the host told?", assumed: "any non-terminal booking, host sees it, not notified"}
  view-usage:
    goal: goal/see-usage
    actors: [persona/member]
    trigger: {actor: persona/member}
    start: show
    ends: {shown: {result: success, means: "member sees own bookings"}}
    steps:
      show:
        actor: persona/member
        choices: {done: {label: Close, then: end/shown}}
        ?open: {question: "What counts as usage history: finished only, or also cancelled? Does it show credits and fees?", assumed: "all own bookings in any state"}
rule:
  own-bookings-only: {kind: holds, scope: {on: workflow, in: [view-usage, add-note]}, body: {statement: "A member sees and changes only own bookings"}}
  no-double-booking: {kind: holds, scope: {on: entity, in: booking}, body: {statement: "No two held or confirmed bookings overlap for one room", check: "derived/room-free"}}
  premium-no-stripe: {kind: guard, scope: {on: step, in: payment}, body: {when: "member.plan = premium", else: step/plan-check},
    ?open: {question: "See payment question above", assumed: "premium skips Stripe"}}
quality:
  usage-history: {of: workflow/view-usage, measure: "time to show", target: value/history-speed, under: "undecided",
    ?open: {question: "Any speed or retention target for history?", assumed: "none"}}
interface:
  member-app: {form: screen, serves: [step/book-room.check-room, step/cancel-booking.check-late, step/add-note.write, step/view-usage.show], version: 1,
    ?open: {question: "Is the member app the only channel (no desk or phone bookings)?", assumed: "yes"}}
```

## Questions for the owner
- How long is a room held while waiting for payment, and what happens to the hold when time runs out?
- What counts as a late cancellation (how close to the start)?
- How much is the late fee: fixed or a share of the price? Does a premium member pay it in credits?
- From what room size does a booking need host approval?
- Where does "waiting for host approval" sit among the four states, and what happens if the host declines or never answers?
- How many credits does a booking cost, and how do premium members get credits?
- Do premium members skip Stripe entirely, and is the booking confirmed at once for them?
- What if a premium member has too few credits?
- Credits are deducted at confirmation: are they given back on cancellation (and on late cancellation)? Is a paid booking refunded on cancel?
- What if the SMS or the Kisi call fails; who is told?
- When does the door code become valid and when does it stop?
- What marks a booking finished (end time or someone's action)? How is the cleaner told, and is there a deadline before the next booking?
- Can a member change a booking (time, room, attendees) or only cancel?
- Can attendees exceed the room capacity?
- How is the price set (per room, per hour)?
- What can the host do beyond approving and reading notes: cancel, see all bookings?
- Until when can a note be added, and is the host notified?
- What does usage history show (cancelled bookings, credits, fees)?
- Are there plans other than premium, and any limits on how far ahead one can book?
- Is the app the only booking channel?
