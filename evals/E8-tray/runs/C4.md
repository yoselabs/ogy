## Questions for the owner

- What exactly makes a room "bigger": capacity cutoff or a per-room flag? What number?
- How long is the room held while waiting for payment, and what happens at expiry?
- Can the host reject a booking (only "approve" was said)? How long may the host take, and what then?
- What counts as a "late" cancel, and what is the late fee (amount or percent)? Is it charged in Stripe or deducted in credits for premium members?
- Are payments refunded on a timely cancel? Are credits returned on cancel (credits are deducted at confirmation)?
- What does a booking cost in money and in credits (per hour, per room)?
- A premium member with too few credits: pay by card or refuse?
- Does a booking awaiting host approval have its own state? You listed only four states (pending payment, confirmed, cancelled, finished).
- Is the room held during host approval, before payment?
- What moves a booking to finished: end of the slot time, or a person's action?
- Can a confirmed booking be cancelled after the meeting started? Can a finished one?
- What if Stripe, the SMS provider or Kisi is down, slow, or answers wrongly or twice? Should the booking confirm if Kisi did not register the code?
- Who notices and fixes a door code that was never texted or never registered? Can the member re-request it?
- Does the door code expire? Does it change if the booking is cancelled (must it be removed from Kisi)?
- How does the cleaner learn about a job; is there a deadline; does the next booking wait for a clean room?
- Who sets up rooms (capacity, approval flag), members (plan, phone number), credit top-ups? Out of scope here?
- Usage history: what does it show (past only, cancelled ones, fees, credits)? Any filtering?
- Notes: length limit, which booking states allow notes, can the host reply, how does the host see it?
- How does the host get asked to approve (app, email, push)?
- Wording of the member-facing texts for each outcome.
