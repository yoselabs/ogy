## Questions for the owner

- What room size or capacity counts as "bigger" and needs host approval, and is it per room or per capacity number?
- Is there a separate state for waiting on host approval, or is it pending payment? The four states you named do not include it.
- How long before the start is a cancellation "late"?
- How much is the late fee, and how is it collected (Stripe, or credits for premium members)?
- Do late fees apply to bookings cancelled while still pending payment?
- How long is a room held while waiting for Stripe payment, and what happens when it lapses?
- How long may the host take to approve, and what happens if the host does not answer?
- Does the member pay before or after host approval?
- How many credits does a booking cost? Per hour or per booking?
- What happens when a premium member has too few credits: pay by Stripe or refuse?
- Are credits refunded on cancellation (free or late)?
- When does a booking become finished: at the end time automatically, or someone marks it?
- Who tells the cleaner, how, and what if the cleaner never marks the room done?
- Does the cleaner need to be told when a booking is cancelled?
- What if the SMS fails or never arrives? Can the member see the code in the app?
- What if Kisi rejects or loses the code? Is the booking still confirmed?
- Must Kisi have the code before the member is texted?
- Are repeated texts or repeated Kisi registrations harmless?
- What if Stripe is down or slow, answers twice, or a payment is refunded or disputed in Stripe?
- What if the paid amount is wrong?
- What does the usage history show (past only, cancelled ones, fees, credits)?
- Can a member add a note after the booking is cancelled or finished? Is there a length limit? One note or many?
- Can the host reply to a note, or see notes only?
- What are the exact messages for each ending, in the app and by SMS?
- How does the host approve (app, email)?
- Can a member change a booking (time, attendees), or only cancel?
- Can a member book a room above its capacity?
- Who creates members, sets plans, phone numbers, rooms and capacities (outside this story)?
