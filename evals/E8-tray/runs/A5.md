# Capture A5: meeting rooms

## Entries

```yaml
persona/member: {nature: human}
persona/community-host: {nature: human}
persona/cleaner: {nature: human}
external/stripe: {owns_facts: [payment status], events: [payment_confirmed]}
external/sms-provider: {owns_facts: [sms delivery]}
external/kisi: {owns_facts: [door codes known to the lock]}

goal/book-room: {states: [a member has a confirmed room for a time slot]}
goal/room-ready: {states: [the room is clean after a meeting]}
goal/see-usage: {states: [a member can see their usage history]}

term/premium-plan: {means: a member plan whose bookings use credits instead of payment}
term/late-cancel:
  means: a cancellation made too close to the booking start
  ?open: {question: how close to start counts as late?, assumed: use value/late-cancel-window, asks: persona/community-host}

set/plan: {members: [standard, premium]}
  ?open: {question: are there plans besides premium and the rest?, assumed: "the rest = standard"}
set/booking-state: {members: [pending_payment, confirmed, cancelled, finished]}

value/big-room-capacity:
  is: ?
  unit: people
  ?open: {question: what makes a room "bigger" (capacity threshold or a per-room flag)?, assumed: a per-room attribute needs_approval}
value/payment-window:
  is: ?
  unit: duration
  ?open: {question: how long is a room held while waiting for payment?, assumed: none stated, hold lasts until paid or cancelled}
value/late-cancel-window: {is: ?, unit: duration}
value/late-fee: {is: ?, unit: money}
value/credits-per-booking:
  is: ?
  unit: credits
  ?open: {question: how many credits does a booking cost (per hour? per room?)?, assumed: one flat number}

entity/room:
  attrs:
    name: {type: text}
    capacity: {type: number}
    needs_approval: {type: bool}
  key: [name]
  ?open: {question: is needs_approval derived from capacity?, assumed: derived: capacity > value/big-room-capacity}

entity/member:
  attrs:
    name: {type: text}
    phone: {type: text}
    plan: {type: set/plan}
    credits: {type: number}
  key: [phone]
  ?open: {question: how are credits obtained and topped up?, assumed: out of scope}

entity/booking:
  attrs:
    room: {type: entity/room}
    member: {type: entity/member}
    start: {type: time}
    end: {type: time}
    attendees: {type: number}
    approved: {type: bool, optional: true}
    note: {type: text, optional: true}
    door_code: {type: text, optional: true}
    late_fee: {type: money, optional: true}
  lifecycle:
    states: [pending_payment, confirmed, cancelled, finished]
    initial: pending_payment
    terminal: [cancelled, finished]
    transitions:
      confirm: {from: pending_payment, to: confirmed}
      cancel: {from: [pending_payment, confirmed], to: cancelled}
      finish: {from: confirmed, to: finished}
  ?open: {question: where does "awaiting host approval" sit among the four states, and is a premium booking pending_payment?, assumed: approval is the bool attr approved; premium bookings skip payment and start pending_payment until credits are deducted}

rule/attendees-fit:
  kind: guard
  scope: {on: workflow/book-room}
  body: {when: "booking.attendees <= booking.room.capacity", else: end/rejected}
  ?open: {question: is attendees above room capacity refused?, assumed: refused}

rule/no-double-booking:
  kind: holds
  scope: {on: entity/booking}
  body: {statement: a room has at most one non-cancelled booking for any time slot}
  ?open: {question: is that right, and do back-to-back slots collide?, assumed: yes, no overlap, back-to-back allowed}

workflow/book-room:
  goal: goal/book-room
  actors: [persona/member, persona/community-host, external/stripe, system]
  trigger: {actor: persona/member}
  inputs: {room: entity/room, start: time, end: time, attendees: number}
  start: step/check-room
  ends:
    confirmed: {result: success, means: booking confirmed and member has door code}
    unavailable: {result: failure, means: the room is not free for that slot}
    rejected: {result: failure, means: host declined or input invalid}
    abandoned: {result: failure, means: payment never came, hold released}
  steps:
    check-room:
      actor: system
      cases:
        free-small: {order: 1, when: "room free and not room.needs_approval", then: step/hold-room}
        free-big: {order: 2, when: "room free and room.needs_approval", then: step/host-approval}
        otherwise: {then: end/unavailable}
    host-approval:
      actor: persona/community-host
      choices:
        approve: {label: Approve, then: step/hold-room}
        decline: {label: Decline, then: end/rejected}
      ?open: {question: is the room held while the host decides, and is there a decision deadline?, assumed: not held, no deadline}
    hold-room:
      actor: system
      causes: [{create: entity/booking}]
      cases:
        premium: {order: 1, when: "member.plan = premium", then: step/deduct-credits}
        standard: {order: 2, when: "member.plan = standard", then: step/await-payment}
        otherwise: {then: end/rejected}
      ?open: {question: when approval is needed, does the member pay after approval?, assumed: yes, approval first as stated}
    await-payment:
      actor: external/stripe
      choices:
        payment_confirmed: {label: Payment confirmed, then: step/confirm}
        payment_failed: {label: Payment failed, then: end/abandoned}
      ?timeout: {after: value/payment-window, then: end/abandoned}
    deduct-credits:
      actor: system
      causes: [{count: entity/member.credits, op: dec}]
      cases:
        enough: {order: 1, when: "member.credits >= value/credits-per-booking", then: step/confirm}
        otherwise: {then: end/rejected}
      ?open: {question: what if a premium member lacks credits: pay by Stripe, or refuse?, assumed: refuse}
    confirm:
      actor: system
      causes: [{transition: booking.confirm}, {write: booking.door_code}, {tell: external/kisi, what: the door code}, {tell: persona/member, what: door code by SMS via external/sms-provider}]
      then: end/confirmed
      ?open: {question: who generates the door code, and when is it valid?, assumed: system generates it, valid for the slot only}
      ?open: {question: what if Kisi or the SMS provider fails?, assumed: booking stays confirmed, retry delivery}

workflow/cancel-booking:
  goal: goal/book-room
  actors: [persona/member, system, external/stripe]
  trigger: {actor: persona/member}
  inputs: {booking: entity/booking}
  start: step/cancel
  ends:
    cancelled: {result: success, means: booking cancelled}
    cancelled-late: {result: success, means: booking cancelled and late fee charged}
  steps:
    cancel:
      actor: system
      causes: [{transition: booking.cancel}]
      cases:
        late: {order: 1, when: "booking.start - now < value/late-cancel-window", then: step/charge-late-fee}
        otherwise: {then: end/cancelled}
    charge-late-fee:
      actor: system
      causes: [{write: booking.late_fee, from: value/late-fee}]
      then: end/cancelled-late
  ?open: {question: can pending_payment and already-started bookings be cancelled?, assumed: pending yes, started no}
  ?open: {question: refund of the payment or credits on an on-time cancel? is the fee charged to premium members, in money or credits? how is it charged (Stripe)?, assumed: full refund on time; fee in money via Stripe; for premium nothing stated}
  ?open: {question: does the Kisi code get revoked on cancel?, assumed: yes}

workflow/finish-booking:
  goal: goal/room-ready
  actors: [system]
  trigger: {after: value/zero, since: transition/booking.confirm}
  ?open: {question: the owner said only "after the meeting"; finish fires at booking end time, is that right?, assumed: at booking.end}
  inputs: {booking: entity/booking}
  start: step/finish
  ends: {finished: {result: success, means: booking finished and room needs cleaning}}
  steps:
    finish:
      actor: system
      causes: [{transition: booking.finish}]
      then: workflow/clean-room

workflow/clean-room:
  goal: goal/room-ready
  actors: [persona/cleaner]
  trigger: {called}
  inputs: {booking: entity/booking}
  start: step/clean
  ends: {ready: {result: success, means: room is ready}}
  steps:
    clean:
      actor: persona/cleaner
      choices: {done: {label: Room is ready, then: end/ready}}
  ?open: {question: how does the cleaner learn about the job, and what if the next booking starts soon?, assumed: told when booking finishes, no deadline}

workflow/add-note:
  goal: goal/book-room
  actors: [persona/member, persona/community-host]
  trigger: {actor: persona/member}
  inputs: {booking: entity/booking, note: text}
  start: step/write-note
  ends: {saved: {result: success, means: note saved on booking}}
  steps:
    write-note:
      actor: system
      causes: [{write: booking.note}, {tell: persona/community-host, what: the note}]
      then: end/saved
  ?open: {question: until when can a note be added or edited, and is the host notified?, assumed: any non-terminal state, host notified}

interface/usage-history:
  form: screen
  serves: [goal/see-usage]
  version: 1
  ?open: {question: what does usage history show (bookings of every state, hours, credits spent, fees) and how far back?, assumed: the member's own bookings, all states}

  of: workflow/book-room

  target: value/door-code-latency
  under: normal load
  ?open: {question: is there any speed expectation?, assumed: none stated, entry is a placeholder}
```

## Questions for the owner

- What makes a room "bigger" and needs host approval: a capacity number or a per-room choice?
- Who is the community host: one person, one per room, or a role anyone can take? What if they don't answer, and is there a deadline?
- Is the room held while the host decides, and is it held for how long after the member is asked to pay?
- Where does "waiting for host approval" fit among pending payment, confirmed, cancelled and finished?
- What if payment fails or never comes?
- What if the room is not free: reject, or offer other rooms or slots?
- Can attendees exceed room capacity? Any minimum or maximum slot length, opening hours, or how far ahead one can book?
- How much is a booking (price per room, per hour)? Where do prices come from? (needed for Stripe)
- How many credits does a booking cost, how are credits bought or topped up, and what if a premium member lacks credits (pay instead, or refused)?
- Are there plans other than premium?
- What counts as a late cancel (how many hours before start), and how much is the late fee?
- Is the late fee charged to the card, to credits, or to premium members at all?
- Are on-time cancels refunded (payment, credits)? Can the member cancel after the meeting started?
- Is a premium booking deducted at confirmation only, and returned to credits on cancel?
- Who makes the door code, how long is it valid, and is it revoked on cancel?
- What happens if Kisi or SMS fails: retry, tell the host, block confirmation?
- When exactly does a booking become finished (at end time)? Who tells the cleaner and how, and how soon must the room be ready?
- What does the usage history show, and how far back?
- Until when can a member add or edit a note, and does the host get notified? Can the host reply?
- Can a host cancel or move a booking, and can two members be in the queue for the same slot?
- Who is a member (sign-up, login) and is phone number the way to reach them?
- Any speed or reliability expectation (door code arrives within N seconds)?
