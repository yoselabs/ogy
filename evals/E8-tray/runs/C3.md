## Questions for the owner

- What makes a room "bigger" and need host approval (capacity number or flagged rooms)?
- Does approval come before payment or after? (I assumed before.)
- How long is a room held while waiting for payment?
- How long does the host have to approve, and what happens if they never answer?
- Is "waiting for approval" a booking state? You listed only pending payment, confirmed, cancelled, finished.
- What counts as a late cancellation (how long before the meeting)?
- How much is the late fee, and how is it charged (Stripe, credits)?
- Do premium members pay a late fee in credits or money? Are credits refunded on cancel?
- Can a pending-payment booking be cancelled, and is there a fee then?
- What if a premium member has too few credits?
- How many credits does a booking cost?
- Is the attendee count checked against room capacity?
- If payment fails or Stripe is down, what does the member see?
- What if the SMS or Kisi fails? Is the booking still confirmed? Which happens first?
- Does the door code expire, and what is its format?
- Refunds on cancel of a paid booking?
- How does a booking become finished (time passing, someone marks it)?
- How is the cleaner told, and what if they do not do it?
- What does the usage history show (credits, payments, fees), and for what period?
- Who can see the note, may it be edited, and its max length? Can it be added after finish?
- Who may cancel besides the member (host, admin)?
- Is the room-free check on the slot alone, or also buffer time for cleaning?
