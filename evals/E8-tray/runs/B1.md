## Questions for the owner

- How late is "late" for a cancellation, and how much is the late fee (flat, percent, per room)?
- What makes a room "bigger" and need host approval: capacity threshold or a per-room flag? What threshold?
- Does the host approval happen before payment or after? What if the host does not respond or declines?
- How long is a room held awaiting payment; what happens when the hold expires?
- What happens if payment fails or Stripe never answers?
- Is "awaiting approval" a booking state? You listed only pending payment, confirmed, cancelled, finished.
- How many credits does a booking cost; what if a premium member lacks credits? Are credits returned on cancel? Do premium members pay late fees, and how?
- On cancel, are payments refunded, early and late?
- How do bookings move from confirmed to finished: slot end time?
- What happens to the door code on cancel and after the meeting (removed from Kisi)?
- If SMS or Kisi fails, is the booking still confirmed? Who finds out the member has no working code?
- What if attendees exceed room capacity: refuse or allow?
- Can members book in the past, or far ahead; is there a slot length rule?
- How does the cleaner learn a room needs preparing and how is "done" recorded? What if they do not?
- Where does the host read the member's note; any length limit; can notes be added after the booking ends?
- What does "usage history" show (dates, room, cost, credits) and may a member see cancelled bookings?
- Who creates rooms, members, plans and phone numbers: outside this scope?
- Who may cancel besides the member (host, admin)?
- Can two members hold the same slot concurrently (race): first to hold wins?
- Are there other plans besides premium?
- Stripe, Kisi and SMS: what do you want when they are down, slow, answer twice, or change things behind our back (refunds, edited codes)?
