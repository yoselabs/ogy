## Questions for the owner

- What is the late-cancellation window (how long before start)?
- What is the late fee amount, and is it flat or a percentage; currency?
- Which rooms count as "bigger" (capacity threshold) and need host approval; what are the room names and capacities?
- Is a room held while waiting for host approval, and is payment requested only after approval?
- What happens if the host declines, or never answers; how long may they take?
- How long is the room held while waiting for payment, and what happens on expiry?
- What if Stripe payment fails or is declined; can the member retry?
- What if Stripe, the SMS provider or Kisi is down, slow, answers twice, or the SMS/code is never delivered?
- If Kisi rejects the code, is the booking still confirmed?
- Is "awaiting host approval" a booking state? Owner listed only pending payment, confirmed, cancelled, finished.
- When does a booking become finished (automatically at slot end)?
- Do premium members with no or too few credits fall back to paying? How many credits does a booking cost?
- Premium members: is a late fee charged in money or credits? Are credits returned on cancel, and when?
- Are paid bookings refunded on cancel (fully, never, depending on time)?
- Can members cancel after the meeting started, or a finished booking? Can unconfirmed bookings be cancelled?
- Who can change a member's plan or credits, and how are credits topped up?
- What if attendees exceed the room capacity?
- How is the cleaner told (and by what interface), is a completion expected, and what if the room is not cleaned?
- What does usage history show (which fields, cancelled bookings, fees, credits used)?
- Can notes be added after confirmation or finish, is there a length limit, can the host reply, and is the host notified?
- Does the member see the door code in the app as well as by SMS?
- Which interface forms exist for member, host and cleaner (web, mobile, email)?
- Exact message wording per outcome and interface.
