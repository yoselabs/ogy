## Questions for the owner

- Which rooms are "bigger" (capacity threshold or a per-room flag)?
- Does host approval come before the hold and payment, or after payment? Is there an extra "awaiting approval" state beyond the four you listed?
- Can the host decline, and how long does the host have before the request lapses?
- How long is a hold kept while payment is pending, and what happens when it lapses?
- What if Stripe fails, is slow, answers twice or never answers?
- What happens when the SMS provider or Kisi fails or is down; is the booking still confirmed? Can the member see the code in the app?
- How long before start counts as a "late" cancel, and what is the late fee (flat or share of price)?
- Does a late fee apply to pending_payment bookings, to premium members, and what if the fee charge fails?
- Are Stripe payments refunded on cancel (early vs late)? Are credits returned on cancel (early vs late)?
- How many credits does a booking cost; may it vary by room or length? What if credits are insufficient?
- Can a premium member pay by Stripe when out of credits?
- Are credits deducted again or restored if a confirmed booking is cancelled by the host or system?
- Who sets the price and what is it (per hour, per room)?
- What does attendee count do beyond approval: is there a capacity limit that rejects the booking?
- Can two members hold the same room and slot; what makes a room "free" (does a pending hold block others)?
- How does the cleaner learn about a room to prepare, and how do they report done? Is the room unavailable until cleaned?
- What moves a booking to finished: the clock after the slot ends, or the cleaner?
- Who can add a note, in which states, how long may it be, and is the host notified or only able to see it?
- What does usage history show (all states, credits, fees) and over what period?
- Can a member change the booking time, room or attendee count after booking?
- How is the door code generated, how long is it valid, and is it per booking or per member?
- How do members and host reach the app: any other interfaces (email, web, dashboard) or notifications beyond the SMS?
- Are there other plans besides premium, and do they differ in approval or fees?
- Who can see or cancel a booking besides the member (host, staff)?
- Message wording for each outcome in each interface.
