# E8-tray capture A4

## Entries

```yaml
# ---- personas, externals, terms ----
persona/member:        {nature: human}
persona/community-host: {nature: human}
persona/cleaner:       {nature: human}
external/stripe:       {owns_facts: [payment-status], events: [paid]}   # failed/expired events not stated
external/sms-provider: {owns_facts: [sms-delivery]}
external/kisi:         {owns_facts: [door-codes]}
term/hold:    {means: "a room-and-slot reserved for a member while payment is awaited"}
term/late-cancellation: {means: "a cancellation made too close to the start of the booking"}   # "too close" is value/late-cancel-window
term/credits: {means: "prepaid units a premium member spends instead of paying per booking"}

# ---- goals ----
goal/book-meeting-room:   {states: ["a member has a room for a time slot, paid for or covered by credits"]}
goal/see-usage-history:   {states: ["a member can see their past use of rooms"]}
goal/ready-room:          {states: ["the room is ready for the next user after a meeting"]}

# ---- sets ----
set/plan:
  members: [premium]
  ?open: {question: "Which other plans exist besides premium?", assumed: "every member not on premium pays per booking"}
set/booking-status: {members: [pending_payment, confirmed, cancelled, finished]}

# ---- values (owner decides; none stated) ----
value/late-cancel-window:
  unit: duration
  ?open: {question: "How long before the start counts as a late cancellation?", assumed: "none; blocks the fee rule", asks: persona/community-host}
value/late-fee:
  unit: money
  ?open: {question: "How much is the late fee, fixed or a share of the price?", assumed: "none; blocks the fee rule"}
value/hold-duration:
  unit: duration
  ?open: {question: "How long is a room held while waiting for payment?", assumed: "no expiry in force"}
value/big-room-capacity:
  unit: number
  ?open: {question: "What makes a room 'bigger' so it needs host approval: capacity above what number, or a flag per room?", assumed: "a per-room flag needs_approval set by the owner"}
value/booking-credit-cost:
  unit: number
  ?open: {question: "How many credits does a booking cost (per hour, per booking, per room size)?", assumed: "none; blocks credit deduction"}

# ---- entities ----
entity/room:
  attrs:
    name: {type: text}
    capacity: {type: number}
    needs_approval: {type: bool}
  key: [name]
  ?open: {question: "Are rooms owned by the app or listed per location; is capacity a hard limit on attendees?", assumed: "single location; attendees above capacity are refused"}

entity/member:
  attrs:
    plan: {type: set/plan}
    credits: {type: number}      # premium only
    phone: {type: text}
  key: [phone]
  ?open: {question: "How is a member identified (account, email, phone) and how do they obtain credits?", assumed: "identified by account; credits are topped up outside this scope"}

entity/booking:
  attrs:
    room: {type: entity/room}
    member: {type: entity/member}
    start: {type: time}
    end: {type: time}
    attendees: {type: number}
    note: {type: text, optional: true}
    door_code: {type: text, optional: true}
    paid_with: {type: text, optional: true}   # stripe | credits
    approved: {type: bool, optional: true}
  lifecycle:
    states: set/booking-status
    initial: pending_payment
    terminal: [cancelled, finished]
    transitions:
      confirm: {from: pending_payment, to: confirmed}
      cancel:  {from: [pending_payment, confirmed], to: cancelled}
      finish:  {from: confirmed, to: finished}
  ?open: {question: "Where does a booking wait for host approval, and is a premium booking pending_payment before credits are taken? The owner lists only four states.", assumed: "approval happens before the booking exists; premium goes straight to confirmed"}

# ---- workflow: book a room ----
workflow/book-room:
  goal: goal/book-meeting-room
  actors: [persona/member, persona/community-host, external/stripe, external/sms-provider, external/kisi]
  trigger: {actor: persona/member}
  inputs: {room: entity/room, start: time, end: time, attendees: number}
  start: step/check-room
  ends:
    booked:        {result: success, means: "booking confirmed and member has the door code"}
    room-busy:     {result: failure, means: "room is not free for that slot"}
    rejected:      {result: failure, means: "host did not approve the booking"}
    not-paid:      {result: failure, means: "payment did not complete"}
    no-credits:    {result: failure, means: "premium member lacks enough credits"}
  ?open: {question: "What happens on payment failure, abandoned payment, host refusal or too few credits? The owner described only the happy path.", assumed: "each is a failure end; booking cancelled and hold released"}
  steps:
    check-room:
      actor: system
      cases:
        busy:     {order: 1, when: "room is not free in [start..end]", then: end/room-busy}
        approval: {order: 2, when: "room.needs_approval", then: step/await-approval}
        free:     {order: 3, when: "true", then: step/hold-room}   # exactly one otherwise
      otherwise: {then: step/hold-room}
    await-approval:
      actor: persona/community-host
      choices:
        approve: {label: "Approve booking", then: step/hold-room}
        reject:  {label: "Reject booking",  then: end/rejected}   # reject not stated by owner
      ?open: {question: "Is approval asked before the hold and payment, or after payment? How long may the host take, and what if no answer?", assumed: "before the hold; no timeout in force"}
    hold-room:
      actor: system
      causes: [{create: entity/booking}, {tell: persona/member, what: "room held; pay to confirm (non-premium)"}]
      cases:
        premium: {order: 1, when: "member.plan = premium", then: step/use-credits}
      otherwise: {then: step/await-payment}
    await-payment:
      actor: external/stripe
      choices:
        paid:   {label: "Stripe confirms payment", then: step/confirm-booking}
        failed: {label: "Payment fails or is abandoned", then: end/not-paid}
      timeout: {after: value/hold-duration, then: end/not-paid}
    use-credits:
      actor: system
      cases:
        enough:   {order: 1, when: "member.credits >= value/booking-credit-cost", then: step/confirm-booking}
      otherwise: {then: end/no-credits}
    confirm-booking:
      actor: system
      causes:
        - {transition: booking.confirm}
      then: step/send-door-code
    send-door-code:
      actor: system
      causes:
        - {write: booking.door_code}
        - {tell: external/sms-provider, what: "text the member the door code"}
        - {tell: external/kisi, what: "register the door code for the room and slot"}
      then: end/booked
      ?open: {question: "Who generates the door code (us or Kisi), how long is it valid, and what if SMS or Kisi fails?", assumed: "we generate it; both sends must succeed, failures retried", asks: persona/community-host}

rule/credits-at-confirmation:
  kind: adds
  scope: {on: transition, in: booking.confirm}
  body:
    causes: [{count: member.credits, op: dec}]   # only when member.plan = premium, by value/booking-credit-cost
  ?open: {question: "Do premium members ever pay with Stripe (e.g. credits run out)? Is credit use a plain decrement?", assumed: "no; credits only"}

rule/premium-no-payment:
  kind: holds
  scope: {on: workflow, in: book-room}
  body: {statement: "A premium member is never asked to pay through Stripe for a booking"}

# ---- workflow: cancel ----
workflow/cancel-booking:
  goal: goal/book-meeting-room
  actors: [persona/member, external/stripe]
  trigger: {actor: persona/member}
  inputs: {booking: entity/booking}
  start: step/classify-cancel
  ends:
    cancelled:      {result: success, means: "booking cancelled, no fee"}
    cancelled-fee:  {result: success, means: "booking cancelled, late fee charged"}
  steps:
    classify-cancel:
      actor: system
      causes: [{transition: booking.cancel}]
      cases:
        late: {order: 1, when: "booking.start - now < value/late-cancel-window", then: step/charge-late-fee}
      otherwise: {then: end/cancelled}
    charge-late-fee:
      actor: system
      causes: [{tell: external/stripe, what: "charge value/late-fee"}]
      then: end/cancelled-fee
  ?open: {question: "Is the payment refunded or credits returned on a timely cancel? Does a premium member pay the late fee (money or credits)? Is the door code revoked? Can a member cancel while pending approval?", assumed: "timely cancel returns payment/credits; late fee charged in money to every member; code revoked"}

# ---- cleaner ----
workflow/ready-room:
  goal: goal/ready-room
  actors: [persona/cleaner]
  trigger: {event: transition/booking.finish}
  inputs: {booking: entity/booking}
  start: step/clean
  ends: {ready: {result: success, means: "room is ready for the next booking"}}
  steps:
    clean:
      actor: persona/cleaner
      choices:
        done: {label: "Room is ready", then: end/ready}
  ?open: {question: "What moves a booking to finished (the meeting end time passing, or someone)? How is the cleaner told, and must the room stay blocked until ready?", assumed: "finish fires at the booking end time; cleaner is notified; room not blocked"}

# ---- note and history ----
workflow/add-note:
  goal: goal/book-meeting-room
  actors: [persona/member]
  trigger: {actor: persona/member}
  inputs: {booking: entity/booking, note: text}
  start: step/write-note
  ends: {noted: {result: success, means: "note stored on the booking for the host"}}
  steps:
    write-note:
      actor: system
      causes: [{write: booking.note}, {tell: persona/community-host, what: "member added a note"}]
      then: end/noted
  ?open: {question: "Until when can a note be added or edited, and is the host notified or does the host look?", assumed: "any time before finished; host sees it on the booking"}

interface/usage-history:
  form: screen
  serves: [goal/see-usage-history]
  version: 1
  ?open: {question: "What must the history show (bookings, hours, credits spent, fees, cancelled ones)? How far back?", assumed: "all of the member's own bookings with status"}
  # no workflow or step serves it yet; goal/see-usage-history is a read-only goal

quality/booking-responsiveness:
  of: workflow/book-room
  ?open: {question: "Any speed or availability expectation (e.g. door code arrives within N seconds of payment)?", assumed: "none stated"}
```

## Questions for the owner

- Which plans exist besides premium, and how do premium members get and top up credits?
- How many credits does one booking cost?
- What counts as a "bigger" room needing host approval: capacity above what number, or flagged per room?
- Is host approval asked before the hold and payment, or after? Time limit, and what if the host refuses or never answers?
- What does the member see when the host rejects a booking?
- How long is a room held while waiting for Stripe payment, and what happens at expiry?
- What if Stripe payment fails or the member abandons it?
- What if a premium member has too few credits: refuse, pay by Stripe, or something else?
- Is a premium booking ever "pending payment", or is it confirmed immediately?
- Does a booking waiting for approval need its own status beyond the four you listed?
- What exactly is a "late" cancellation (how long before start)?
- How much is the late fee, and does a premium member pay it in money or credits?
- On a timely cancel, is payment refunded or credits returned? On a late cancel, is any of it refunded?
- Can a member cancel a booking waiting for approval or payment, and after the meeting started?
- Is the door code revoked on cancel?
- Who generates the door code (us or Kisi), how long is it valid, and one per booking or per member?
- What if the SMS or Kisi send fails: retry, tell the host, block confirmation?
- Is attendee count above room capacity refused? Can attendees be changed after booking?
- Can a member change the room or time of a booking, or only cancel and rebook?
- What turns a booking into "finished" (the end time passing, or a person)?
- How is the cleaner told, and must the room stay unbookable until the cleaner is done?
- What does the cleaner do if the room cannot be made ready?
- Who may see the note (host only?), can it be edited, and until when?
- What must the usage history show, how far back, and who else may see it (e.g. the host)?
- Does the community host see all bookings, and is there one host per space or per room?
- Any expectations on speed or reliability (e.g. door code arrives promptly after payment)?
